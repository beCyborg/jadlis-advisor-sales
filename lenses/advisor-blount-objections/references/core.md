# advisor-blount-objections — core principles, ranked by evidence

The layer's claim is not "this method works". It is that the method's components rest on very unequal footings, and a council answer must say which rung it is standing on. Ranked strongest → weakest by the ladder token attached in `moves.md`.

Ceiling note: the source book's evidence ceiling is `external-research`, and only three citations in the entire book reach it — and all three support *background premises*, not the sales moves built on top. Nothing in this layer is `field-study`. Any move presented as proven is a demotion candidate.

---

## Tier 1 — `external-research` (three items, all background premises)

**1. Decision is emotional before it is rational — so answer the feeling first, then the logic.** `[OBJ:EMOTION-FIRST]`
The premise cites Damasio's work on patients with limbic damage who retain reasoning but cannot decide. What the citation supports is that affect participates in choice. What it does *not* support is the specific sequencing recipe (relate → isolate → minimize → ask) or any claim about conversion. The recipe on top of the premise is `practitioner-n-many` at best. Practical consequence for a council answer: "resolve the emotion first" survives challenge; "and therefore run these five steps in this order" does not.

**2. People find self-disclosure intrinsically rewarding — so questions plus silence outperform talking.** `[OBJ:SELFDISC]`, and by extension `[OBJ:DEEPLISTEN]`, `[OBJ:CLARIFY-OPEN]`
Mitchell and Tamir's PNAS fMRI work on reward-region activation during self-disclosure is named. This is the strongest single support in the layer and it holds up the layer's most reusable behavior: the seller shuts up and the buyer explains their own objection. Everything else in this tradition degrades gracefully; this one component is why the layer is worth consulting at all. Still an extrapolation — nothing in that research measured a sales outcome.

**3. Posture and physical state feed felt confidence — partially, and with a documented casualty.** `[OBJ:POSTURE-SHIFT]`
Cuddy's power-posing work is cited by name. The hormonal arm (testosterone/cortisol) failed replication after the book's sources were written; only the self-reported felt-confidence effect survives. This principle is therefore *demoted in place*: usable as a nudge, never as a mechanism, and it should be flagged as such whenever a user quotes it back. See `myths-and-demotions.md`.

## Tier 2 — `practitioner-n-many` (the load-bearing body of the layer)

These rest on the author's training practice across cohorts and industries: repeated observation by an interested party, no dataset, no control.

**4. Objections occur at every request point, not at the close.** `[OBJ:TYPE-SORT]`, `[OBJ:RBOTAX]`, `[OBJ:CLASSIFY-RQN]`
The layer's single most defensible structural contribution. Four types (prospecting reflex/brush-off/true objection · red herring · micro-commitment brush-off · buying commitment), each with its own response mode, chosen from the *type* rather than the wording. This is what the layer sees that rebuttal-library thinking cannot: the same sentence ("it's too expensive") means four different things depending on where in the cycle it lands, and three of those four are not close-stage problems at all.

**5. A late objection is a symptom, and the diagnosis is upstream.** `[OBJ:PROCESSDISCIPLINE]`, `[OBJ:BRUSHOFFDIAG]`
Skipped discovery, unmet stakeholders, an unbuilt case for change, an ask made without earned context. The corollary is a coaching rule: when someone asks "what should I have said?", the honest answer is usually about a meeting three weeks earlier. This principle is the reason the layer distrusts its own Stage 6 content — see the ordering rule at the end of this file.

**6. Classification must precede response; the rep's own state must be regulated before either.** `[OBJ:LEDGE]`
Cognitive capacity collapses exactly when the answer is needed, so every framework opens with a pre-memorized pause rather than with content. The ledge is a mechanical device for buying the time to classify. Its neuroscience framing ("magic quarter second") is popular press, not research — the *practice* is n-many, the *explanation* is `assertion`.

**7. Argument hardens the buyer; agreement disarms them.** `[OBJ:RBO-DISRUPT]`, `[OBJ:RELATE]`, `[OBJ:LIKE-MOST]`, `[OBJ:NONCOMP-BEHAVIOR]`
"Overcome / combat / rebut" is rejected as a category. Confronting a buyer with proof their current vendor is failing triggers a defense of their original decision. The reliable inversion is to ask what they like about the incumbent and let the complaints arrive unprompted, because criticism the buyer authored themselves does not need defending against.

**8. Isolate before you minimize.** `[OBJ:ISOLATE]`, `[OBJ:PRIORITY-ECHO]`, `[OBJ:CLARIFY-OPEN]`
Answering the first objection before knowing whether it is the only, real, or biggest one produces an endless objection queue. Order is load-bearing in a way the rest of the five-step sequence is not: the isolate/clarify pair is `practitioner-n-many` and reusable; the full five-step choreography around it is `practitioner-anecdote`.

**9. Banked yeses are the raw material of the close.** `[OBJ:YES-LEDGER]`, `[OBJ:MICRO-LADDER]`, `[OBJ:TRUTHEARLY]`
With no explicit agreements collected during discovery, the minimize step has nothing to work with. This is the layer's own admission that the close-stage machinery is downstream of discovery quality — and the clean test for whether Stage 6 moves are even available in a given deal. The commitment/consistency mechanism cited underneath is second-hand and unmeasured in a sales context.

**10. Silence after the ask; no self-inflicted objections.** `[OBJ:ASK-SHUTUP]`, `[OBJ:NO-SELF-INFLICT]`
Post-ask talking, answering unasked questions, pre-empting objections "to get them on the table", importing objections from other deals. Cheap to verify — most of it is visible in a transcript, which makes it the layer's best call-review checklist and the reason it earns a seat in CALL mode.

**11. Ask directly and assumptively; hedged asks manufacture resistance.** `[OBJ:ASSUM-ASK]`, `[OBJ:ASSUM-LANG]`, `[OBJ:RE-ASK]`
Note the tension with the ethics contour: the supporting ratio (50–70% vs 10–30%) is self-reported by the author's firm and must not be quoted as fact. The behavioral instruction — name a specific action and date instead of requesting permission — stands on its own without the number.

**12. Control the meeting frame before content; park red herrings rather than solving them.** `[OBJ:PAIS]`, `[OBJ:FRAMECONTROL]`, `[OBJ:AGENDACHECK]`, `[OBJ:FIVEMINFLIP]`
The documented failure mode this prevents is concrete and specific: a price question answered before a demo turned into a group pile-on that the meeting never recovered from. Everything in this cluster is the author's career default, presented as observation. The sub-claim that parked objections "almost never" resurface is personal observation only.

**13. Widen the stakeholder map before the objection arrives from someone you never met.** `[OBJ:BASIC-MAP]`, `[OBJ:BOSS-SPLIT]`, `[OBJ:DMINDIRECT]`
Influencers are named as the main source of blindside objections. The BASIC taxonomy is proprietary and has no outcome data behind it — treat it as a checklist, not a model.

**14. Diagnose the funnel, not just the sentence.** `[OBJ:YESNUMBER]`, `[OBJ:PROB-WALK]`, `[OBJ:PIPE-DETACH]`
Separate efficiency from effectiveness, move one lever at a time, and check the neighbouring ratio. The layer's own cautionary case: a client doubled proposal-to-close and still missed revenue because lead volume had collapsed underneath.

**15. Prepare adversarially before the call.** `[OBJ:MURDER-BOARD]`, `[OBJ:REHEARSE]`, `[OBJ:PRECALL-4Q]`, `[OBJ:PRE-RESEARCH]`
Murder boarding is explicitly a confirmation-bias countermeasure, which means it fails when run alone — the seller cannot be their own challenger. That self-aware caveat is the strongest thing about it; the effectiveness claim itself is unmeasured.

## Tier 3 — `practitioner-anecdote` (one story, one dialogue, one number)

These may be *useful* and are still the weakest rung short of assertion. A council answer should never let them carry the recommendation alone.

- **The full five-step buying-commitment sequence** `[OBJ:5STEP-FRAME]` — formalized from a single reconstructed story in which the author was the buyer. Its components (relate, isolate) rank higher separately than the choreography does as a whole.
- **The two-turnaround stop rule and the ~50% second-RBO rate** `[OBJ:TWORBOMAX]` — rules of thumb. Useful as a discipline precisely because they cap persistence, not because the numbers are known.
- **Deadline math, cost-of-inaction arithmetic, burden shifting, peer bridges** `[OBJ:DEADLINE-MATH]`, `[OBJ:COST-OF-INACTION]`, `[OBJ:BURDEN-SHIFT]`, `[OBJ:PEER-BRIDGE]` — one worked example each, all with the author's own numbers.
- **Proof assets** `[OBJ:PROOF-BINDER]`, `[OBJ:PROOF-HARVEST]` — the author's own binder and his own routine, self-reported as near-always effective.
- **Hang-up recovery** `[OBJ:HANGUPRECOV]` — anchored on one war story (52 daily voicemails, callback on day 53). Survivorship-shaped: the failed cadences left no story.
- **Confirmation call, rule of thirds, this-or-that, self-talk reset, post-ask silence** `[OBJ:CONFIRM-CALL]`, `[OBJ:THIRDS]`, `[OBJ:THISORTHAT]`, `[OBJ:SELFTALK-RESET]`, `[OBJ:ASK-SHUTUP]` — personal practice, explicitly framed as what the author sees.

## Tier 4 — `assertion` (no support offered)

- **Priming and scarcity effects claimed for the meeting objective** `[OBJ:AGENDA-OBJECTIVE]` — illustrated with generic word-cue examples, no named study, no sales measurement. This is the single lowest-ranked move in the layer and should carry an explicit caveat whenever it is proposed.
- **The mechanism narrations attached to otherwise n-many moves**: amygdala/novelty detection under `[OBJ:PATTERN-PAINT]`, "people want what they cannot have" under `[OBJ:TAKEAWAY]`, availability under `[OBJ:AVAIL-TOUCH]`, illusory truth and frequency illusion under `[OBJ:PREFRAME-COMP]`, ambiguity bias under `[OBJ:LESS-IS-BETTER]`, reciprocity under `[OBJ:AGENDAOPEN]`. The behaviors sit in Tier 2; the neuro/bias explanations underneath them do not, and per the lens spec, brain-based rationalizations are refused by design.
- **Guarantee language** — "follow the process and you will close more, guaranteed" `[OBJ:PRECALL-4Q]`, and the closing certainty attached to `[OBJ:PROCESSDISCIPLINE]`.

---

## Ordering rule this layer applies to itself

When both are available, an upstream move outranks a downstream one regardless of tier: `[OBJ:PROCESSDISCIPLINE]` and `[OBJ:BASIC-MAP]` before `[OBJ:5STEP-FRAME]`; `[OBJ:TRUTHEARLY]` before `[OBJ:RE-ASK]`. The reason is internal to the tradition, not imported: the layer's own diagnosis is that a rebuttal at the close is what you are left with when the earlier work was skipped.

Second rule: a stop rule outranks a turnaround. `[OBJ:TWORBOMAX]`, the reasons 5–7 branch of `[OBJ:BRUSHOFFDIAG]`, and `[OBJ:PROB-WALK]` are the correct output whenever the objection names a real constraint — a signed contract, a frozen budget, an absent authority, a compliance block. The emotional-first machinery has nothing to operate on there, and persisting past it is the layer's own documented failure mode.
