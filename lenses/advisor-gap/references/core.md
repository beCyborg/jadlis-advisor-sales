# advisor-gap — core

The principles of the диагностика layer, ranked by how well each is supported, not by how
central the tradition treats it. Ladder, strongest to weakest:
`field-study` → `external-research` → `practitioner-n-many` → `practitioner-anecdote` → `assertion`.

**Nothing in this lens reaches `field-study`.** The book's ceiling is `practitioner-n-many`:
one consultant's practice across client B2B teams, self-reported. Everything below is
sorted inside that ceiling, with the relayed third-party research kept separate — a
borrowed statistic supports the *premise* the author builds on, never the *method* he
built on top of it.

---

## Tier 1 — rests on relayed external research (premise only, never the method)

These are the only load-bearing claims in the lens with data behind them, and in every
case the data is somebody else's, cited second-hand, about the surrounding market — not
about gap selling.

- **A B2B decision is not one person's.** The multi-stakeholder premise under
  [GAP:BUYPROC], [GAP:YESMAP] and [GAP:REDISCOVER] comes from the relayed Gartner/CEB
  figure of at least 5.4 decision makers per B2B sale (a single decision maker plausible
  only under roughly $1,000). Strong as a premise; the mapping routines built on it are
  practitioner-grade. Corollary that follows cleanly: discovery does not transfer between
  people in the same role — [GAP:REDISCOVER] is the cardinal rule of the layer, and its own
  support is one lost deal.
- **Sellers who talk less in discovery are found in won-deal recordings.** [GAP:TALKRATIO]
  leans on a relayed Gong.io analysis of a very large recorded-call corpus (lost-deal
  discovery: buyers ~28% of talk time; won-deal discovery: sellers listening past half).
  This is a directional correlation observed by a third party, not a test of this method,
  and it cannot separate "asking more" from "the deal was already good." Treat as the
  strongest thing in the lens and still not causal.
- **Change resistance has a finite named taxonomy.** [GAP:THREATSCAN] borrows Kanter's ten
  threats wholesale. The list is external; its application here is illustrated with
  invented vignettes.
- **Everything else the book cites is vendor-published.** The Sales Executive Council /
  Challenger profile split and the OMG / Kurlan need-to-be-liked figures under
  [GAP:EXPERT] come from firms selling the conclusion. Usable as an argument, not as
  evidence.

## Tier 2 — practitioner-n-many (the method's actual spine)

Repeated across the author's client implementations, self-reported, never measured
against a control. This is where the lens's real content lives — treat as usable
mechanism, not as validated causation.

- **The sellable object is arithmetic, not persuasion.** Gap = quantified future state −
  quantified current state, netted against the *total* cost of change (price +
  implementation + process disruption + risk) — [GAP:GAPMATH]. The layer's whole claim to
  distinctiveness is that it produces a number a buyer can audit instead of a feeling a
  seller can assert.
- **Current state has five separable elements, and four of them are usually skipped.**
  Facts, problems, impacts, root causes, emotional state — [GAP:CS5]. A deal described in
  product terms has none of them; [GAP:DIAGFIRST] and the CRM Challenge ([GAP:CRMCHAL],
  [GAP:CRMCOACH]) exist as cheap tests for whether the seller actually holds them.
- **Problems ladder; impacts are unique; root causes are finite and reusable.**
  [GAP:LADDER3] (technical → business → existential) plus [GAP:ROOTCAUSE]. This is the
  layer's most transferable structural claim: technical problems repeat across prospects,
  business problems do not, so selling to the business layer is what stops you sounding
  like every other vendor. [GAP:PIC] is the operational form — the root-cause column is
  finite, which is why it can be pre-built.
- **Qualification is an output of discovery, not a gate before it.** [GAP:NOBANT] and
  [GAP:QUAL4]: fixable problem exists / buyer agrees / buyer wants it fixed / buyer will
  work the fix with you. Budget, authority and timing are deliberately deferred. The
  reasoning is coherent; the claim that this beats B.A.N.T. is untested, and it is the
  single move most likely to be wrong in a high-volume pipeline.
- **Vague answers are not data.** [GAP:NOOPEN] plus [GAP:FSQUANT] — every stated problem,
  impact and goal must resolve to a figure and a timeframe, or the gap is fictitious and
  collapses under procurement scrutiny. [GAP:BASICFACT] is the cheap version: the missing
  base quantity that reorganises the whole account.
- **Deals advance on buyer commitments, not seller activity.** [GAP:NEXTYES] and
  [GAP:NEXTYESMGT]: name the single next yes the *buyer* must give, and its value to
  them. Rising seller effort with flat buyer effort is the diagnostic for a dead deal;
  [GAP:STALLALARM] operationalises it (same next yes for a month = intervene).
- **Disqualification is a first-class outcome.** [GAP:WIDEN], [GAP:GRACEEXIT],
  [GAP:BUDGVAFF]'s "cannot afford → walk". Framed as both ethics and pipeline hygiene.
  This is the strongest ethical guardrail the tradition offers, and it is entirely
  voluntary — see myths-and-demotions.md.
- **Objections are tested against the buyer's own stated outcome, never against the
  product.** [GAP:OBJDEFEND], [GAP:PRICEFRAME], [GAP:CRITALIGN]. The mechanism depends
  wholly on having quantified numbers from the buyer; without them each of these is a
  bluff, which the book itself concedes.
- **Demo is a consequence of discovery, not a stage.** [GAP:NODEMO], [GAP:NOIF],
  [GAP:DEMOMAP], [GAP:SIXMAX]. The "if" test is the cleanest self-diagnostic in the whole
  layer: a conditional in the demo means a hole in the discovery.
- **Management is the survival condition, not an add-on.** [GAP:PIPE7], [GAP:FACTSONLY],
  [GAP:REVIEWBOX], [GAP:RATEMATH], [GAP:HEALTHCHK], [GAP:COMMIT15], [GAP:COACHCULT]. The
  author states outright that solo adoption is fragile and the method decays without
  managerial reinforcement — which is honest, and also exactly what a training vendor
  would want to be true.
- **Outreach is priced from the buyer's side.** [GAP:OFFERMINUSASK] — treat the requested
  minutes as money spent. A named formula with worked examples, but self-scored: a
  discipline, not a measurement. [GAP:PROBFIRST], [GAP:IMPACTROTATE], [GAP:CADENCE12] and
  [GAP:CHANMIX] all sit under it, the last on the author's own unpublished survey.

## Tier 3 — practitioner-anecdote (one story, generalised)

Real mechanisms, single illustrations. Use them; do not cite them as proof.

- **The stated need is a hypothesis, not a brief** — [GAP:NOTNEED], resting on one retail
  story (charger vs. case).
- **Problems chain downstream** — [GAP:PROBCHAIN], one dialogue.
- **Latent demand can be surfaced by walking the process with volumes** —
  [GAP:DEMANDCREATE], one scripted ladder. This is simultaneously the layer's most
  powerful and most abusable routine.
- **Asking the distance to their own plan reframes a self-diagnosed problem** —
  [GAP:GAPDISTANCE] and [GAP:WHYDRIVE], both from the same single client account
  (ARMS Reliability), so they are one data point, not two.
- **An unmapped approver kills a sold deal** — [GAP:YESMAP], the author's own failed
  startup; [GAP:GAPFLIP] is the same case read again.
- **Discovery does not transfer between peers** — [GAP:REDISCOVER], one lost deal.
- **Persistence pays** — [GAP:NEVERNO], [GAP:LATECHAL], recollections with no base rate for
  the buyers who were annoyed and never said so.
- **Cost-of-waiting arithmetic moves budget deferrals** — [GAP:COSTOFWAIT], one recalled
  deal, outcome self-reported.
- **Executives ask a fixed set of questions of every vendor** — [GAP:BUYERLETTER], one
  letter from one executive.
- **Expertise gaps can be opened deliberately** — [GAP:KNOWGAP], [GAP:SURPRISE],
  [GAP:PROBFIRST], single illustrations each.

## Tier 4 — assertion (the author's confidence, nothing else)

Named here so the council never treats them as lens content with weight. Several are
structurally important to the method, which is precisely the problem.

- **[GAP:FSEMO] — future-state emotional anchoring.** Presented as the magic of the method.
  Zero support. It is also the method's main manipulation surface: future-pacing on an
  unverified gap manufactures urgency. Highest ratio of structural importance to evidence
  in the entire lens.
- **[GAP:FS3LAYER] — three future-state layers.** A structuring heuristic with no cases.
- **[GAP:ANCHORQ] — affirming questions anchor value.** A generic cognitive-bias claim, no
  named study.
- **[GAP:GAPSUBJ] — subject-line intrigue.** The supporting neuro-framing (four-second
  verdict, "oh-shit circuit", ACC/error-related negativity) has no citable source and is
  refused outright by the lens's ethical contour: neuro-rationales are REFUTED by design.
  The move may still be usable; its stated reason is not.
- **[GAP:EMAILSCORE] — the scorecard.** Author-designed rubric, no calibration, no outcome
  data. A checklist, never a send gate.
- **[GAP:RFPOUT] — closed RFPs are out of scope.** Asserted as a boundary of the method with
  no comparative win-rate data. Honest as a limitation, unevidenced as a decision rule.
- **[GAP:TONECAL], [GAP:TALKERTELL], [GAP:WALKAWAY]** — craft observations and a
  reputational argument.
- **The whole hiring chapter — [GAP:NINETRAIT], [GAP:NOKNOWALL], [GAP:HIREQS].** No
  validation study, Dunning-Kruger invoked by name without citation, and the claim that
  eight of nine traits are untrainable is a strong assertion about adult development with
  nothing behind it. Also self-serving by construction: the hiring criterion is the
  author's own method.

## What the ranking implies for council use

1. When this lens contradicts a lens whose layer rests on field data, **it yields on the
   mechanism and keeps only its diagnostic checklist.** Practitioner-n-many does not
   outrank a measured study, however confidently the tradition states it.
2. **The gap is a real object; the conversion is not.** [GAP:GAPMATH] is the lens's
   contribution and its blind spot at once — the method never measures how often an agreed
   gap becomes a purchase. Any forecast built on it ([GAP:COMMIT15], [GAP:HEALTHCHK])
   inherits that untested step.
3. **Cost per opportunity is unpriced.** [GAP:CS5] + [GAP:NOBANT] + [GAP:PIPE7] together are
   only economic above mid-ACV. Below that the lens should say so rather than prescribe.
4. **Ethical guardrails are voluntary.** [GAP:WIDEN] and [GAP:GRACEEXIT] are the only checks
   on [GAP:FSEMO], [GAP:PROBCHAIN] and [GAP:DEMANDCREATE], and nothing external enforces
   them. The book states plainly that a seller can move the perceived size of the gap in
   either direction.
