# advisor-klaff-frame — core

The frame/status layer of the deal, ranked by how well each component is actually supported. Both sources are by one author (Klaff, 2011 and 2019); their agreement is a single school talking to itself, never independent confirmation. The whole layer's evidence ceiling is `practitioner-anecdote`, so nothing below claims more.

Ranking runs strongest → weakest on the ladder: `field-study` → `external-research` → `practitioner-n-many` → `practitioner-anecdote` → `assertion`. This layer has **no** components at `field-study` or `practitioner-n-many`. That absence is the headline: there is no component of this method that rests on a counted sample, a control, or a reported failure case.

---

## Tier 1 — `external-research` (the construct exists outside the books; the books did not establish it)

These are the only principles the layer can lean on without borrowing the author's credibility. In each case the *construct* is established elsewhere and the *books' evidence for it* is not; use the construct, discard the books' claimed support.

1. **Reactance — buyers push back against felt steering.** The single most load-bearing principle in the 2019 book: pressure, artificial urgency and discount scripts produce the opposite of compliance. Reactance is an established social-psychology construct (Brehm); the book cites it loosely and attributes supporting studies to unnamed manufacturers. Everything in the no-ask family rests here: [FRAME:NOASK], [FRAME:NO-TRIAL-CLOSE], [FRAME:NONEEDY], [FRAME:POSTPITCH], [FRAME:HOLDSILENCE], [FRAME:WITHDRAWCLOSE].
2. **Ownership bias — people price and defend conclusions they believe they generated.** The stated success condition is inversion: the buyer proposes the deal back to you. Endowment and self-generation effects are genuinely studied; the book's evolutionary origin story for them is not, and is demoted. This is the foundation of [FRAME:BUYERFORM], [FRAME:BUYERASIDE] and the entire handover step of [FRAME:SEQUENCE].
3. **Processing fluency — lower cognitive load reads as more credible.** Directionally supported in the fluency literature, cited by neither book. It underwrites [FRAME:MSG-SIMPLIFY], [FRAME:ARCHETYPE] and [FRAME:IDEA-INTRO], and it is in visible tension with the 2011 book's own correction that over-simplifying reads as naive — hold both and let the audience decide which applies.
4. **Schema-driven rapid judgment.** Stereotype and schema effects on fast social judgment are real behaviourally; the "receptor" hardware story around them is invented and demoted. Supports [FRAME:ARCHETYPE] and [FRAME:STATUSTIP] as behavioural heuristics only.

**What Tier 1 does not license.** None of these constructs was tested by the books in a sales setting. They establish that the *direction* of these moves is plausible, not that the scripts work, not how often, and not at what cost.

## Tier 2 — `practitioner-anecdote` (one operator, self-reported, no counter-cases)

This is where the bulk of the layer lives. Each item below is supported by one or a handful of narrated deals, told by the person who executed them, after the fact, with the winning interpretation attached.

5. **Frames collide rather than blend; the directional test is who is reacting.** The operational diagnostic — if you have to explain your authority, you do not have it — is the most portable idea in the layer and the least verified. Whole stage C and D rest on it: [FRAME:NO-RITUAL], [FRAME:STATUS-SEQ-6], [FRAME:PLOW], [FRAME:DEFY-TIME], [FRAME:EASYONBUYER].
6. **Status is situational and manufacturable inside a narrow domain.** Global status (wealth, title) is fixed; local authority inside a slice you genuinely own is buildable and evaporates on leaving the room. [FRAME:LOCAL-STAR], [FRAME:FLASHROLL], [FRAME:FLASHTHEIRS], [FRAME:MARKETFLASH]. The whole family carries one hard boundary: it reads as mastery only to someone less prepared than you.
7. **Peer-rank sorting gates attention before content does.** The claim that a decision-maker allocates attention by perceived rank rather than by likeability, delivered through insider vocabulary plus a recent consequential action plus a live open issue. [FRAME:TIPOFF], [FRAME:TIPOFFSTORY], [FRAME:PEERENTRY], [FRAME:STATUSALIGN].
8. **Buyers stall from a certainty gap, not an information gap.** More logos and more answers widen it; a short flat demonstration of mastery closes it. This is the most operationally useful claim in the 2019 book and it is supported by exactly two narrated deliveries. [FRAME:FLASHROLL], [FRAME:FLASHEXIT], [FRAME:PRESTRESS], [FRAME:DEATHRATTLE].
9. **Novelty buys attention and destroys commitment.** The larger the decision, the more conservative the criteria; concentrate all strangeness into one named difference and then show it is now standard. [FRAME:NOVCHUNK], [FRAME:VANILLASCRIPT], [FRAME:NEWNORMAL]. Note that the same mechanics that soothe a conservative buyer can bury a defect — see `myths-and-demotions.md`.
10. **Prizing — invert the qualification direction so the buyer works for access.** [FRAME:PRIZE-OPEN], [FRAME:PRIZE-INT], [FRAME:PRIZE-EXT], [FRAME:QUALIFY-BACK-EARLY], [FRAME:QUALIFY-BACK-LATE], [FRAME:QUALTRIAD], [FRAME:EARN-TASK]. **Precondition the books never test:** every prize move presupposes real alternatives, and neither book supplies a way to check whether you actually hold them, nor a fallback for when you do not.
11. **Neediness is situational, and the cure is behavioural.** Pre-loaded exit, one excellence demonstration, planned withdrawal, and silence after the last line. [FRAME:NOWANT], [FRAME:POSTPITCH], [FRAME:DISENGSCAN], [FRAME:SELF-TIMEBOX].
12. **Attention is a decaying resource inside a fixed time budget.** The roughly twenty-minute pitch, four phases at 5 / 10 / 2 / 3 minutes, background under two minutes, mechanism capped at ten. [FRAME:CUTHOT], [FRAME:STRONG-SEQ], [FRAME:SECRET-SAUCE], [FRAME:OFFER-CONCRETE], [FRAME:SELF-TIMEBOX]. The timings are one operator's habit, not a measured curve.
13. **Attention needs interest *and* stakes.** A pitch with only upside gets polite silence; adding something to lose restarts engagement. [FRAME:PING], [FRAME:NOVELTY-INJECT], [FRAME:INTRIGUE-STORY], [FRAME:PUSHPULL-3LEVEL], [FRAME:TAKEBACK]. The two-chemical explanation for this is demoted; the behavioural pattern survives.
14. **Threat → reward → fairness as the pre-detail sequence.** Structural threat first, a measurable doubling second, demonstrated personal exposure third. [FRAME:BIGTHREE], [FRAME:WINTER], [FRAME:DOOMCONTEXT], [FRAME:TWOX], [FRAME:SKIN]. The ordering is the author's; the "receptor" framing behind it is demoted to a name.
15. **A pitch is a sequence with discrete failure points, and the diagnosis is which step broke.** [FRAME:STRONG-SEQ] (2011) and [FRAME:SEQUENCE] (2019) are two versions of the same instinct from the same author — treat their similarity as one voice, not two.
16. **Moral authority as the frame that outranks status and price.** [FRAME:MORAL-AUTH-STANDARD], [FRAME:MORAL-AUTH-STEWARD], [FRAME:MORAL-AUTH-RIGHT]. Also the layer's most reputation-damaging component to fake, by the books' own admission.
17. **"Always be leaving" replaces "always be closing".** Withdrawal as the terminal move: [FRAME:WITHDRAWCLOSE], [FRAME:EXITSIGNAL], [FRAME:MRBIG-15], [FRAME:LOVEORLEAVE]. Credible only with a real exit; run while desperate it is theatre the buyer reads accurately.

## Tier 3 — `assertion` (stated as a rule, no case attached even inside the books)

These are components the reader may reasonably discard first. They are ranked lowest not because they are wrong, but because nothing in either book supports them beyond the author saying so.

18. **Rapport contributes nothing to rank, and researching the buyer personally is wasted time** — [FRAME:NORAPPORT]. This runs directly against mainstream sales research on trust-building; it is the single most portable-looking and least portable claim in the layer.
19. **The recognisable sales repertoire marks you as subordinate** — [FRAME:AVOID-BETA-TRAP]. A sweeping claim about decades of sales training with no data on which techniques still convert.
20. **Force the frame collision early** — [FRAME:MOMENTUM-EARLY]. Stated as a rule; the punctuality half is uncontroversial, the collision half is not.
21. **Buyer power frames break easily and should not be pressed once broken** — [FRAME:EASYONBUYER]. A general observation about buyer behaviour.
22. **Withdrawal on disengagement rather than addition** — [FRAME:DISENGSCAN]. A behavioural rule resting on the author's reading of rooms.
23. **The three gating buyer questions are the fixed hierarchy** — [FRAME:BIGTHREE]. Attributed to unnamed psychologists, no study, sample or citation.
24. **Constraints installed gradually go unnoticed** — [FRAME:SLOWFENCE]. The supporting attention research does not license the claim and is demoted; the tactic remains an assertion.
25. **Scarcity limits generate their own pull** — [FRAME:ONEPER]. Asserted as universal, and in direct tension with the same book's condemnation of fake-scarcity scripts.
26. **Buyers who insist on running their own process are bad customers** — [FRAME:DISQUALIFY]. A self-serving test: it classifies well-informed, price-comparing buyers as the problem.
27. **A flat exit from the expertise burst is mandatory** — [FRAME:FLASHEXIT]. A delivery rule with no case.
28. **The half-and-half payment split is the normal arrangement** — [FRAME:HALFHALF]. The transaction volume attributed to it is given with no source.

## Structural properties of the whole layer

- **No base rate anywhere.** Neither book reports a single case where one of its own moves lost a deal. Outcomes are narrated by the executor, after the fact.
- **No attribution within a deal.** The 2011 case study fires five or more frames simultaneously and concedes the losing competitor might have won on other grounds. No component is isolable.
- **No stop rule on the escalating moves.** [FRAME:PLOW], [FRAME:PUSHPULL-3LEVEL] and the status sequence have escalation instructions and only vague retreat conditions. The most damaging moves have the least specified exit.
- **Single-use by construction.** [FRAME:TIPOFF], [FRAME:TIPOFFSTORY], [FRAME:FLASHROLL], [FRAME:FLASHTHEIRS], [FRAME:ALLOWNO], [FRAME:EXITSIGNAL], [FRAME:REOPEN], [FRAME:PARTNERSKEPTIC], [FRAME:CURIOSITY] and [FRAME:INTRIGUE-STORY] depend on the counterpart not having seen them. Neither book models the second encounter, the renewal, or the buyer who has read the book.
- **Buyer-side cost is unmodelled.** Meetings burned, relationships strained and reputational spend are never counted. The author's economics — few, enormous deals — make those costs invisible in a way they are not for volume sellers.
- **Committees and process are barely present.** Champions, procurement, security review, legal and budget cycles exist as an audience to be moved, never as a process to be navigated.
- **No post-sale horizon.** Several components create obligations the deal must later honour — [FRAME:SKIN]'s commitments, [FRAME:LOCALSTAKE]'s local promises, [FRAME:BIGCOSMALL]'s senior staffing — and neither book has any account of what the seized status costs in month six.
- **Seller-side prerequisites are assumed, not stated.** Real domain depth, principal-level discretion, deal flow to walk away and a bench to staff. A quota-carrying rep at a large vendor has none of them, and the layer offers such a seller almost nothing.
- **The explanatory layer is discarded entirely.** Every neuro-mechanism used in either book to justify these principles is refuted, overstated or fabricated (see `myths-and-demotions.md`). Each principle above stands or falls on the evidence line attached to it, and nothing else.

## Where the layer's own claims contradict each other

- [FRAME:MSG-SIMPLIFY] instructs hard simplification; the same book's debunking section attacks "simplicity always wins" and says relationship detail should stay rich. Unresolved inside the source.
- [FRAME:ONEPER] runs a scarcity limit; the 2019 book lists scarcity scripts among the legacy pressure tactics it condemns. Unresolved inside the source.
- [FRAME:TIME-TRAIN] adds a deadline while the same book concedes that time pressure reduces decision quality — an argument against its own technique.
- The 2011 book concedes the whole apparatus is unnecessary when the product is already irresistible. Read plainly: this layer is a remedy for an under-differentiated offer pitched to a distracted principal, not a general theory of selling.
