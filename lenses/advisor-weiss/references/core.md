# advisor-weiss — core principles, ranked by evidence

Layer: **консалтинг: цена от ценности**. Source: Alan Weiss, *Value-Based Fees* (2021), `[VBF:]`.

**Ladder ceiling for the whole lens: `practitioner-n-many`.** There is no field study, no external research, no control group and no failure sample anywhere in this tradition. Every rate, split and multiple is the author's recollection of his own practice at enterprise ACVs with a single economic buyer. So the ranking below is not "proven → unproven"; it is "repeatedly observed by one practitioner → observed once → asserted". Treat even the top tier as a strong practitioner prior, not evidence.

---

## Tier 0 — `field-study` / `external-research`

**Empty. Deliberately so.** The only external citation in the source (a US average architect hourly wage, Salary.com 2020) is background colour for an argument against hourly billing, not evidence for any technique. Any claim from this lens that sounds like a finding — close rates, option-selection splits, "80% repeat business" — is self-report. If the council needs a measured claim about pricing, this lens cannot supply it and should say so.

---

## Tier 1 — `practitioner-n-many`: the load-bearing mechanisms

These are the parts that survive if you strip out the register. Each is a repeated operating procedure in one long practice, described consistently and with worked cases.

1. **The fee is a function of buyer-stipulated value, not of your inputs.** `[VBF:PASTFUTURE]` is the whole method in one exercise: your background and your delivery mechanisms are inputs; only the client's future result is priceable. If your quote moves when the number of days, sites or participants moves, you are pricing column two.

2. **Sequence is causal, not cosmetic.** `[VBF:SEQUENCE]` — shared values, then a peer relationship, then conceptual agreement, and only then a number. `[VBF:FIVE-GATES]` restates it as a hard precondition list. The claim carrying the weight: skipping a link removes the *input* pricing depends on, so a fee named early is arithmetic on missing data, not a negotiation error.

3. **Conceptual agreement = objectives + measures + value, in that order, with the economic buyer only.** `[VBF:CONCEPTAGREE]`, operationalized by the question banks `[VBF:Q-OBJECTIVES]`, `[VBF:Q-MEASURES]`, `[VBF:Q-VALUE]`. The load-bearing constraint is *whose mouth the number comes from*: value you compute and the buyer nods at is not stipulated value, and it will not survive the price conversation. `[VBF:VALUEDENSITY]` and `[VBF:VALUE-COLLAB]` are the density and capture rules on top.

4. **Options convert a yes/no into a how-much.** `[VBF:OPTIONS3]` is the single most transferable mechanic in the lens and the one most often mangled: options must differ in *value*, not in quantity of the same thing. `[VBF:UNBUNDLE]` supplies the raw material; `[VBF:NOFEESYET]` protects the ordering (value preference forms before price is visible).

5. **Never introduce a time unit.** `[VBF:NOHOURS]`. The mechanism claim — a rate card caps profit at hours × rate, rewards slowness, and invites procurement to compare — is sound accounting. The *ethics* claim wrapped around it ("hourly billing is unethical") is assertion and should be dropped when speaking to a user who bills hourly for legitimate reasons.

6. **Commitment beats compliance as a fee predictor.** `[VBF:COMMITGATE]`. Buyers who co-own the project pay more and blame less; easy agreement and fast delegation to HR/finance/legal are treated as buying-signal look-alikes that actually predict a low, contested fee. `[VBF:SHAREDWIN]` is the delivery-side version.

7. **Resistance sorts into four lanes: trust, need, urgency, money.** `[VBF:FOUR-FILTERS]`. The useful half is diagnostic discipline — answering the objection you are comfortable with instead of the live one is a named failure mode. The weak half is the money lane; see Tier 3.

8. **Objections after conceptual agreement are answered by removing value, never by discounting.** `[VBF:REMOVEVALUE]`, `[VBF:QUIDPROQUO]`, `[VBF:NO-DEAL-UNASKED]`. The reasoning is anchor management: a fee that drops without a scope change retroactively announces the original number was padded.

9. **Access is a sellable good, structurally separate from projects.** `[VBF:ACCESS-IS-VALUE]` + `[VBF:PROJECT-SEPARATION]` + `[VBF:RETAINER-10]`. The separation test — if the work needs more than your advice and counsel, it is a project — is the part that actually prevents retainers from decaying into unpaid delivery.

10. **Deliverables are the wrong noun.** `[VBF:TASK2OUT]`, `[VBF:NODELIVER]`, `[VBF:METHODLINE]`. Artifacts are easy to describe, easy to compare, and therefore easy to discount. Reframing each activity as the client result it produces is the same move applied to proposals, site copy and early-meeting language.

11. **Qualitative outcomes are priceable if the judging contract is set in advance.** `[VBF:QUALMETRIC]` (six parameters, buyer as judge, graded not binary, time-bound) and `[VBF:BOARDTEST]` (how would you prove it to your board / to a journalist). This is the lens's best answer to "the value is real but unmeasurable" — and it fails exactly when the judge is not the economic buyer.

12. **Payment terms are part of the price.** `[VBF:TERMS-FIRST]`, `[VBF:EXPENSE-DISCIPLINE]`, `[VBF:BUYER-CHANNEL-DEFENSE]`. Prepayment discount, acceptance-in-lieu-of-signature, expenses billed monthly, and keeping procurement to paperwork only. Time-to-cash is treated as a first-class metric, not an afterthought.

13. **Conversion of a legacy hourly book is selective and event-triggered.** `[VBF:CONVERTTRIAGE]` (score first, convert the top, abandon the bottom), `[VBF:CIRCUMSTANCE]` (attach the change to a trigger), `[VBF:GROUNDWORK]`, `[VBF:RESISTLOOP]`. The explicit instruction not to convert everyone at once is the part most often ignored.

14. **Expansion runs through new economic buyers inside the account.** `[VBF:LATERALBUYERS]`, `[VBF:SCOPE-CREEP-PROPOSAL]`. Every new buyer is educated as a fresh client — never inherited onto the legacy hourly basis.

15. **One ethical constraint holds against the author's own interest:** `[VBF:COACHCONTRACT]` — when the payer is not the coaching subject, the reporting arrangement is fixed and disclosed up front, and progress is never reported without the subject's consent. It is the only rule in the source that costs the consultant something, which is itself diagnostic about the rest.

## Tier 2 — `practitioner-anecdote`: single-case mechanisms, use with a named caveat

Directionally usable, but the support is one story and often a favourable one. Do not present these to a user as "what works".

- `[VBF:HALFTIME]` — double the fee, halve the volume. One poolside conversation. The self-esteem framing ("nobody would pay that" is a symptom, not data) is unfalsifiable as stated.
- `[VBF:EXPERTBOUNDARY]` — declining to be told how you work. One Mercedes-Benz selection process; survivorship-shaped.
- `[VBF:ALLOCATION-ASK]` — asking for the allocation. One case ($150K allocation, options rebuilt to $128K/$141K/$172K).
- `[VBF:OVERBUDGET]` — one option above the stated budget. Asserted from practice; the supporting story is a *lost* deal reinterpreted.
- `[VBF:PHASE-REBATE]`, `[VBF:START-NOW-BRIDGE]`, `[VBF:MESSYBOX]`, `[VBF:BILLINGSYSTEMFIX]` — one worked case each. `[VBF:START-NOW-BRIDGE]` in particular reports the deal that closed, not the ones where the bridge extended unpaid exposure.
- `[VBF:BUYER-CHANNEL-DEFENSE]`'s central story (the purchasing agent later fired) is an unusually favourable single outcome standing in for a policy.
- `[VBF:PRICE-PRESENTATION]`, `[VBF:SOLO-PREMIUM]`, `[VBF:PREPAY-LADDER]`, `[VBF:COACHVSADVISOR]` (including the 90-day coaching cap), `[VBF:SITUATIONAL-FEE]`, `[VBF:CHARGE-THE-FREE]`, `[VBF:SUBSCRIPTION-FLIP]`, `[VBF:ULTIMATE-CONDUCT]`, `[VBF:HOW-CAN-I-HELP]`, `[VBF:ASK-GUIDANCE]`, `[VBF:BEST-PRACTICE-BROKER]`, `[VBF:PROPOSAL-AS-CONFIRMATION]`, `[VBF:BRANDLIFT]`, `[VBF:DIAGNOSE-TOGETHER]`, `[VBF:PEERPUSH]`, `[VBF:NOCONTINGENCY]`, `[VBF:WALK-AWAY]`, `[VBF:MENTOR-OBJECTIVE-CRITERIA]`, `[VBF:NO-DEAL-UNASKED]`, `[VBF:ALUMSURVEY]`, `[VBF:ADVISOR-DEMAND-GEN]`, `[VBF:UNBUNDLE20]`, `[VBF:PRODUCT-PRICE-HIGH]`, `[VBF:TERMS-FIRST]`, `[VBF:EXPENSE-DISCIPLINE]`.

`[VBF:BACKWARDROI]` sits awkwardly across tiers: the eight-step scaffold is a repeated procedure (Tier 1 in form), but every constant inside it — the 20:1 divisor, the +20% option step, the uniqueness uplift, the ±15% sanity band — is an unvalidated round number. Use the *shape*, discount the *constants*.

## Tier 3 — `assertion`: rhetoric, house rules and levers with no support

Keep these out of any claim the council makes on the user's behalf. Several are also where the method's manipulation risk concentrates.

- `[VBF:CREATE-URGENCY]` — the manufactured-urgency lever list, no cases. The buyer's *personal* career stake is a lever the source never flags as a conflict between the buyer's interest and the organization's. This lens's ethical contour: do not deploy any urgency lever you would not state openly to the buyer.
- `[VBF:SMACK-COMPARISON]`'s embarrass-the-buyer mechanism — asserted, and the relationship risk is underweighted. The comparison library itself is fine as *information*; humiliation as a closing device is not.
- `[VBF:MONEY-IS-PRIORITY]` — "money can always be found" is an absolutism. The mechanism (budgets are reallocated, not pre-existing) is defensible; the universal claim is not.
- `[VBF:INBOUNDWEIGHT]`, `[VBF:NO-DEAL-VIBES]`, `[VBF:STOPWORK]`, `[VBF:YEAR-END-PREPAY]`, `[VBF:PROPPROTECT]`, `[VBF:RETAINER-EXIT]`, `[VBF:RESENTMENT-TRIGGER]`, `[VBF:PRO-BONO-INVOICE]`, `[VBF:RETAINER-PROJECT-DISCOUNT]`, `[VBF:PHASE-LOCATE]`, `[VBF:PORTFOLIO-HYGIENE]` — house rules stated as laws, with round numbers (20%/33% in-retainer discount, bottom-15% pruning, biennial +10%) that have no outcome data behind them.
- `[VBF:TWO-TACTICS-MONTH]` — the attached "double your profits in a year, 4–5× with growth" projection is the single most inflated number in the source. Never quote it.

## What the whole lens rests on, stated as a falsifiable condition

The model holds when **four things are simultaneously true**: (a) there is one person who owns the outcome, the budget and the value estimate; (b) that outcome is measurable, or at least judgeable against a pre-agreed contract `[VBF:QUALMETRIC]`; (c) the stipulated value is large enough that a 10–20:1 conservative divisor still yields a fee worth the discovery cycle; (d) you have enough differentiation that `[VBF:WHYME3]` returns honest yeses.

Remove any one and the method degrades in a specific way, not gracefully:
- no single buyer → `[VBF:CONCEPTAGREE]` has no one to stipulate value; the lens has no committee mechanism and does not admit the gap;
- no measure → no denominator, and the source's own logic silently falls back to time-based pricing;
- low ACV → the arithmetic, not the philosophy, breaks: `[VBF:BACKWARDROI]`, `[VBF:ROIFLOOR]` and `[VBF:OPTIONS3]` produce numbers no buyer entertains, and the discovery cycle costs more than the deal;
- no differentiation → `[VBF:QUOTE-HIGH-REDUCE-VALUE]`, `[VBF:HALFTIME]` and `[VBF:ULTIMATE-CONDUCT]` are unbacked posture, and the source's own Ch.7 attack on specialization contradicts the rest of the book on exactly this point.

Everything the lens says should be read through that four-condition gate before it is offered to a user.
