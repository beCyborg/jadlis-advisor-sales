# advisor-enns — Myths and Demotions

What this tradition debunks, what it demotes, and what inside it must itself be demoted. Three sections: claims the lens attacks, documented failure modes of its own techniques, and numbers that are self-reported and must never be quoted as fact.

---

## 1. Myths this lens attacks (use these against the user's framing when it fits)

**"Full service / we do everything."** Read by buyers as a low-integrity claim, because broad expertise is known to be hard to amass. Breadth is treated as an integrity problem, not a capability advantage; "broad expertise" is called an oxymoron [WWP:FOCUS3], [WWP:NARROWCLAIM].

**"We differentiate on our people, our process, our passion, our price."** Treated as non-differentiation. Chemistry and enthusiasm are tiebreakers at best, and leading with them concedes that on expertise you are interchangeable [WWP:PASSIONHOLD].

**"The customer is always right."** A retail idea wrongly imported into expertise work. Accepting the buyer's self-diagnosis is named as the root of bad engagements: a dominant client directs work from an unexamined diagnosis, then the "we only did what we were told" spiral into mutual blame follows. Doctors, lawyers and accountants are singled out as the worst clients precisely because taking control is trained into them [WWP:SELFDIAG].

**"Win as a vendor now, get promoted to expert later."** Roles are assigned during the sale and status traded for money rarely returns. Winning a pitch is still a loss, because the judge-and-auditioner dynamic persists into delivery and caps what you can deliver and charge [WWP:SAMPLE-CONDUCT], [WWP:STATUS-FIRST].

**"We don't do spec creative."** False comfort while strategy and diagnosis are still given away. Free thinking, not free design, is the actual leak [WWP:NOFREETHINK].

**"Thin margins now, better margins later."** Rejected outright: margin and power only decay. The recovery plan is called naive. Superior service does not create profit; profit creates the room to fix mistakes, so the least profitable clients receive the worst service [WWP:NODISCOUNT], [WWP:PRICELOSS].

**"A signed contract means they're committed."** Commitment escalates private decision → spoken → signature → payment, and only payment counts [WWP:DEPOSIT].

**"They asked for a proposal, so they're ready to buy."** The named costliest error of the tradition: interest read as intent [WWP:INTENTTEST]. A written proposal is decoded as a device that lets the buyer avoid saying no.

**"They said they have full authority."** People systematically understate the approvals above them; no access during the sale predicts no access during delivery [WWP:DM-OPEN-NARROW].

**"The disappearing client ghosted us."** Post-proposal silence is attributed to the seller's own neediness having made honest refusal socially expensive [WWP:MAGIC-EMAIL], [WWP:P-EQ-DBD].

**"Our job is to explain our value."** Even nominally economic value is personal; three people who agree on outcomes, metrics and economics can still prefer different vendors and prices for reasons each will rationalize as organizational [WWP:FOUR-Q], [WWP:PERSONAL-MANDATE].

**"Weak roster? Add more clients."** The roster problem is quality, not quantity; the fix is turnover managed upward [WWP:CHURNPLAN].

**Change orders are safe because they're small.** Firms are fired over small tactical invoices, not large strategic ones.

**"The euphoria after signing means it's done."** The buyer's high fades within hours into doubt, and the seller's instinct — more excitement, big-picture language — is exactly wrong where calm, sequence and specifics are needed [WWP:3MODES], [WWP:PROCESSDOC].

**Scripts and memorized lines.** Treated as evidence you are preparing a monologue; frameworks are visible structures, scripts are vendor behavior [WWP:VC-RECAP].

**Rebutting your own objection after raising it.** A self-inflicted signal of neediness [WWP:PREEMPTOBJ].

**Good/Better/Best and Gold/Silver/Bronze naming.** Rejected as persuasion smuggled in at the end of a sale spent not persuading [WWP:NAME-EXPLAIN].

**Closing speed as a proxy for deal quality.** The cheap option closes faster precisely because it raises fewer questions [WWP:NOUPSELLONYES].

**Procurement's hard-nosed posture after an option is selected** is described as leverage-free theater.

**"An elegant proposal will persuade in our absence."** A one-page document will not rescue a champion nervous about asking for money [WWP:INSISTONJOINTPITCH].

**"The one who eddied out."** Early success from a boom or one benefactor client removes the pressure to specialize; the reckoning arrives later and is misread as bad luck or a bad market.

## 2. Anti-recommendations and documented failure modes of this lens's own techniques

Each of these is a way the lens's own moves go wrong. Cite them when the user is already doing the technique.

**Refusal theatre without expertise behind it.** Retreat only works if the buyer values what withdraws. A firm with no differentiated claim that performs [WWP:PURSUENO] or [WWP:RETREAT-ADVANCE] simply loses the deal. Same for [WWP:MINIMUM] used as posture — the source asks directly what happens when they call the bluff.

**Waiving an unstated boundary is not selectivity, it is not having parameters.** A minimum never spoken cannot be traded [WWP:MLEWAIVE].

**Falling in love with disqualifying.** Rigorous qualifying degenerates into intellectually lazy refusal; poor fit today is not poor fit forever, and a third way often exists [WWP:DECLINE-FIT], [WWP:REASSURE].

**The mantra as swagger.** [WWP:MANTRA] recited by someone whose personal bias is the need to be respected produces an arrogant know-it-all, which the source names as the failure path.

**Extrapolating the power formula into hidden or suppressed desire.** Minimizing expressed desire reads as aloof and arrogant; [WWP:P-EQ-DBD] is calibration, not coldness.

**An invented process collapses under questioning.** [WWP:PROCESSDOC], [WWP:DIAGPROC] and [WWP:MODEL-WALK] all fail loudly if the methodology was assembled for the sale — improvisation in front of an anxious buyer reads as danger.

**A perspective used as bait.** If the way-of-working map is indistinguishable from competitors', the client learns the ideology was marketing [WWP:JOURNEY-MAP], [WWP:IDEOLOGY].

**Ideology aimed at people or organizations rather than ideas and conventions** is called poison by the source itself. A perspective that resonates with everyone is meaningless; first-mover advantage from one decays as it goes mainstream.

**The concession ladder pushed too far.** The client's reconciliation flips from "different" to "demanding and annoying" [WWP:CONCESSION-LADDER].

**Over-optimistic scoping of the cheap option.** Named as widely destructive; and never show an option you would resent delivering [WWP:BUDGET-HONEST], [WWP:BUDGETLITERAL], [WWP:NOUPSELLONYES].

**Incentive framing used to shrink the visible anchor** defeats the anchor's purpose [WWP:BIG-NUMBER], [WWP:PERF-PAY].

**The anchor as pressure.** The source defends the anchor as an invitation the client may decline, but never examines when a deliberately non-palatable top number crosses into pressure, nor what happens to a buyer unable to read the structure. The lens must supply that check itself: if the user would not show the buyer the reasoning behind the anchor, do not run it [WWP:ANCHOR-CLOSED], [WWP:GUARANTEE-ANCHOR].

**Pre-empting a budget objection to trigger a defensive "of course we can afford you."** The tactic works because it engages the buyer's self-image; the source frames it as honest early filtering and never examines the misuse case [WWP:PREEMPTOBJ].

**The guaranteed-case probe misheard as a promise.** [WWP:GUARANTEE-ANCHOR] requires repeatedly resetting the hypothetical inside the same exchange; a buyer who loses the thread hears a guarantee that was never offered.

**The Magic Email as a lever.** Sent early or used to manufacture a reply, [WWP:MAGIC-EMAIL] is manipulation rather than release.

**Standard "overcoming objections" training** is subordinated here to keeping the dynamic two-way — but the lens has no answer at all for a buyer who is purely price-shopping, which the first source concedes explicitly.

## 3. Demoted inside the tradition: numbers and claims that are self-reported

None of the following may be quoted as evidence. All are author- or publisher-supplied, with no external verification.

- **Circulation of the 2010 manifesto reported as over 100,000 copies** — publisher self-report, and in any case not evidence the method works.
- **The Minimum Level of Engagement at roughly 10% of annual target fee income** — no derivation given [WWP:MLE].
- **Deposit of one third to one half of the fee portion, or the whole first-phase fee** — given as appropriate practice with no stated basis [WWP:DEPOSIT].
- **The qualifying range with a top around 5× the bottom, illustrated as $250K / $50K** — one example, not a rule with data [WWP:RANGE-5X].
- **The minimum-price dialogue figures $10K / $25K / $50K / $100K** — illustrative dialogue [WWP:MINIMUM].
- **The full worked value scenario** — 36% → 50% share, a ~$450m market, ~$4.5m per share point, ~$63m topline, ~20% category margin rounded to ~$10m value, a $2m anchor, a $1m guaranteed-case counter, a $100k approved budget, the $2m → $1m → $750k pricing path, a $425k middle reasoned down to $350k against a $225k cost floor, $250k profit removed leaving $500k to scoping, a $50k phased step, terms of $60k over 12 / $22.5k over 3 / $10k prepaid — **a single self-reported scenario**, quoted throughout the second source as if it were a template [WWP:ORDER-MAG], [WWP:WALK-DOWN], [WWP:PROFIT-OFF-TABLE], [WWP:SPEC-MIDDLE], [WWP:TERMS-LEVER].
- **Utilization profit threshold of roughly 60–70%** — asserted [WWP:PROFIT-OFF-TABLE].
- **Comparators attributed to unnamed well-known consulting firms** — some charging ~50% of one year of value, some up to 100%, others regarding 10% as too high — entirely unsourced.
- **"Three options produce 75% positive outcomes versus 50% for one option."** This is arithmetic on outcome labels, not measured data [WWP:THREE-OPT].
- **Competitive odds framed as "below 1/n" versus "better than 1/2"** once you hold the inside track — offered without derivation [WWP:INSIDETRACK].
- **The claim that the model is taught across 12+ fields of expertise** — self-reported by the training business that sells it.
- **Claimed effects of the one-page, three-option proposal** — higher closing ratio, lower cost of sale, rising average proposal value [WWP:ONE-PAGE], [WWP:FINAL-SWEEP]. Asserted with no measurement.
- **Silence tolerance growing from about three seconds to much longer with practice** [WWP:SILENCE] — self-reported.
- **Needs assessment of 8–15 questions in ~20 minutes; a 30-minute ask for missing seniors; 10 minutes as the compromise ask; 3 bids as the typical compliance requirement** — conventions from the author's practice, not norms with data [WWP:NEEDS-ASSESS], [WWP:REGROUP], [WWP:POLICYLANG], [WWP:PROCESS-Q].

### Borrowed science that is not verifiable in the sources

**Anchoring, extremeness aversion and the paradox of choice** are used as load-bearing mechanisms via endnotes that do not resolve to verifiable named sources in the text. **The Transtheoretical Model** is applied to buying by analogy, which the second source itself flags as outside what that model was built for. Two ideas are acknowledged as borrowed with unknown origin: the "sale is the sample" phrasing and the Closing the Loop email. Use the tactics; never present the psychology as established.

### Structural demotion of the tradition itself

- **The two books are one author and one training business.** Their agreement is continuation, never corroboration. Any verdict that stacks "both Enns books say X" as two votes is defective.
- **The four-stage sequence is conceded by its own author to be a construct** — neither reliably linear nor discrete, defended as useful rather than true.
- **Unfalsifiable by construction.** Failure of any proclamation is explained by failure of an earlier one, so the system cannot be disconfirmed from inside. This is the single most important demotion in the file: it means a bad outcome will always be diagnosed as "you did it wrong."
- **Survivorship.** The buyers described tolerated the posture. Neither book says anything about deals lost to firms that simply answered the RFP, and there is no way to compare.
- **The absolutes are a persuasion device.** Manifesto-genre statements ("free pitching is free thinking, period") are the author's stance aimed at changing firm-owner behavior, not measured practice. Read every rule as a strong default with its own stated exceptions.
