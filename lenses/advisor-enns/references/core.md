# advisor-enns — Core

The method of the **expert position** layer, ranked by how well each component is supported — not by how central it is to the tradition. The lens ceiling is `practitioner-anecdote`; nothing here reaches `practitioner-n-many`, `external-research` or `field-study`, because neither source reports win rates, price realization, cohorts or controls. Both books are by the same author, who sells training built on this material, so agreement between them is one system stated twice.

Read the tiers as a weighting instruction: Tier 1 claims may be argued in a verdict as "observed repeatedly in one practice"; Tier 4 claims must be labelled as the author's stance whenever they are used.

---

## Tier 1 — practitioner-anecdote: repeated observation inside one advisory practice

Strongest support available in this lens. Still a single consultant's client base in creative, marketing and consulting firms, with no counterfactual and heavy survivorship (the buyers described are the ones who tolerated the posture).

1. **Interest is not intent, and confusing them is the costliest recurring error.** A buyer who asks for a proposal before intent has formed is still shopping for inspiration; the test is a date anchored to a real event that survives the question "why" [WWP:INTENTTEST]. Named by the author as the mistake he sees most often across firms — the closest thing in this lens to a repeated-observation claim.
2. **Buyers arrive with a self-diagnosis and it is usually wrong at the level that matters.** Clients ask for X and need Y; the working response is neither to accept nor argue but to propose finding out [WWP:SELFDIAG], with the desired-future-state question doing the real work [WWP:THREE-YEAR-Q].
3. **People understate the approvals above them.** The decision-maker map is where information is most often missing, so the question is asked open-then-narrow and never skipped [WWP:DM-OPEN-NARROW]; the surprise attendee at the closing meeting is the decision maker missed in qualifying [WWP:ROLL-CALL].
4. **Behavior is a better read on your position than words.** Stated preference is suspect; a granted concession is evidence [WWP:CONCESSION-LADDER], [WWP:REVEALED-PREF], and someone usually holds the inside lane already [WWP:INSIDETRACK].
5. **A documented, visible process functions as reassurance late in the cycle.** Detailed "how will this work" questions are anxiety, not information-seeking, and are answered with a codified model [WWP:PROCESSDOC], [WWP:MODEL-WALK].
6. **A paid first phase resolves the free-proposal problem.** Selling diagnosis as phase one converts unpaid discovery into work [WWP:PAIDDIAG], and the same instrument fills a missing baseline [WWP:SELL-DIAGNOSTIC] or de-risks a large engagement [WWP:PHASE-BELOW].
7. **Value quantification can proceed on order-of-magnitude arithmetic done aloud.** Clients will ratify a rounded figure they will not disclose precisely [WWP:ORDER-MAG] — though the client's own numbers are taken at face value, which neither book treats as a risk.

## Tier 2 — assertion, but argued from a stated mechanism and illustrated with dialogue

The mechanism is explicit and falsifiable in principle; nobody tested it.

8. **Power in the sale is a function of substitutes.** Non-substitutability (narrow, provable expertise) plus willingness to walk is the only durable lever; everything downstream — no free thinking, minimum fees, no presentations — is plumbing of this one idea [WWP:FOCUS3], [WWP:NARROWCLAIM]. This is the load-bearing beam of the whole lens and it is argued, not measured.
9. **The sale is a working sample of the engagement, and roles set during it persist.** Conduct during selling assigns expert or vendor status, and status traded for money rarely returns [WWP:SAMPLE-CONDUCT], [WWP:STATUS-FIRST]. Presented as a founding premise, illustrated by counterexample.
10. **Expressed desire is inversely related to power** (stated as P = Db/D): calibrate your expressed enthusiasm just under theirs, keep visible investment low, and let no be available [WWP:P-EQ-DBD], [WWP:NOOVERINVEST], [WWP:HEAR-NO], [WWP:SAY-NO]. A formula with no measurement behind the ratio; the retreat-and-follow dynamic that carries [WWP:PURSUENO] and [WWP:RETREAT-ADVANCE] rests on an aphorism.
11. **Value must be set before solutions, and price before scope.** The four questions — outcome, metric, worth, willingness to pay — run to completion before any solution talk, which disconnects price from cost [WWP:FOUR-Q], [WWP:ANCHOR-CLOSED], [WWP:PROFIT-OFF-TABLE]. The ordering rule is the lens's most operational contribution and its most confidently asserted.
12. **Conversation beats presentation because presenting assigns the roles of judge and auditioner** [WWP:NOPITCHMEET], [WWP:CONVERSE-NOT-PRESENT]. Note the corollary the author states himself: winning a pitch is still a loss, because the dynamic persists into delivery.
13. **Objections are cheaper raised by you than by them** [WWP:PREEMPTOBJ], followed by silence rather than self-rebuttal [WWP:SILENCE]. The ethical edge — that pre-empting a budget objection works by engaging the buyer's self-image — is named nowhere in the sources and must be supplied by the lens.
14. **Commitment escalates and only payment counts**: private decision → spoken → signature → payment [WWP:DEPOSIT]. Thresholds (one third to one half) are given as appropriate practice with no basis.
15. **Diagnose before prescribe, in four phases** — diagnose, prescribe, apply, reapply — with competitive pitching structurally destroying the diagnostic phase [WWP:FOURPHASE], [WWP:NOFREETHINK], [WWP:DIAGPROC]. The phase model is an analogy from medicine.

## Tier 3 — assertion: normative rules with no stated mechanism

Treat these as the author's defaults with his own exceptions attached, not as findings.

16. **Refusal is the only lever the seller permanently owns**, so selectivity moves — minimum fee floors, declining on fit, walking away — are the system's spine [WWP:MLE], [WWP:MINIMUM], [WWP:DECLINE-FIT], [WWP:4PRIORITIES]. Every one of them presumes you can afford the deal to die.
17. **Price is a positioning instrument, not a cost calculation**: round numbers over hours, more for larger organizations, occasional losses on price as the calibration signal [WWP:BIGROUND], [WWP:SIZEPRICE], [WWP:PRICELOSS], [WWP:NODISCOUNT], [WWP:DISCRULES]. No pricing data anywhere.
18. **Positioning decays advisor → partner → supplier unless actively defended**, which is why churn is normal and turnover is managed upward rather than avoided [WWP:CHURNPLAN], [WWP:PRICETEST].
19. **Policy language beats preference language** [WWP:POLICYLANG] — reported as encountering less resistance, never compared.
20. **Delivery rules follow from the sale**: strategy agreed before creative, input on strategy traded for freedom on execution, the work presented only by the firm [WWP:STRATFIRST], [WWP:EXECFREEDOM], [WWP:WEPRESENT]. Stated as firm policy; [WWP:WEPRESENT] does not scale past the principal.
21. **Adapt frameworks, never principles** [WWP:BENDFRAMEWORKKEEPPRINCIPLE] — a universal standard offered without support, and the clause that makes the system hard to disconfirm from inside.
22. **The buying-mode model** (help the unaware, inspire the interested, reassure the committed) [WWP:3MODES], [WWP:INSPIRE], [WWP:REASSURE]. The author's own construction; its underlying behavior-change staging is imported by analogy from health research the second source concedes was not built to describe buying.

## Tier 4 — borrowed psychology, thinly sourced: use the move, drop the justification

These carry real operational weight in the lens and the weakest support in it. The sources invoke published psychology through endnotes that do not resolve to verifiable named studies in the text. **Use the tactic if it fits; never cite the science.**

23. **Anchoring** as the reason to name a large figure first and to say the anchor price aloud before the document appears [WWP:ANCHOR-CLOSED], [WWP:PRICEBEFOREPAPER], [WWP:GUARANTEE-ANCHOR].
24. **Extremeness aversion and choice overload** as the reason for exactly three options and a middle framed as the safe zone [WWP:THREE-OPT], [WWP:MIDSAFEZONE], [WWP:WALK-DOWN]. The "three options give 75% positive outcomes versus 50% for one" argument is arithmetic on outcome labels, not data.
25. **The claimed downstream effects of the one-page proposal** — higher closing ratio, lower cost of sale, rising average proposal value [WWP:ONE-PAGE], [WWP:FINAL-SWEEP]. Asserted, unmeasured, and not to be repeated to a client as fact.
26. **The Closing the Loop email** [WWP:MAGIC-EMAIL] — origin unknown even to the author, two reply patterns reported, no response rates.

## Where the components contradict each other

The tradition is not internally consistent across its two books, and the lens must not present it as if it were.

- **Written vs. spoken proposal.** The 2010 book says never write a proposal; the document is the contract [WWP:ORALPROP]. The 2023 book builds a whole method around a written one-page, three-option proposal [WWP:ONE-PAGE], [WWP:THREE-OPT]. This is a revision, not a range of tools — choose deliberately.
- **Minimum stated early vs. deployed late.** [WWP:MLE] says state the annual floor in the first discussion; [WWP:MINIMUM] calls the minimum a jarring hurdle and warns against reaching for it early or reflexively.
- **Always recommend vs. never advocate.** [WWP:FEWOPTIONS] requires a recommendation with every set of options shown; [WWP:UNATTACHEDFACILITATE] forbids steering and permits a recommendation only when invited [WWP:ASKTORECOMMEND]. The delivery-side and sale-side rules are opposites.
- **Kill the opportunity early vs. do not fall in love with disqualifying.** [WWP:PURSUENO] and [WWP:HEAR-NO] push toward early death; [WWP:DECLINE-FIT] warns that rigorous qualifying degenerates into intellectually lazy refusal.

## The circularity to watch

Both books explain a failed proclamation by the failure of an earlier one: you could not hold the price because positioning was weak, positioning was weak because you never made the focus decision. That structure makes the system nearly impossible to falsify from inside, and it makes "you did it wrong" the default diagnosis for any bad outcome. When this lens is asked why a deal was lost, it must name a check that could have come out the other way — otherwise the verdict is unfalsifiable by construction.

## Preconditions the whole method assumes

State these explicitly whenever the lens is applied, because every ranked component above silently depends on them.

- A **defensible narrow claim with proof already behind it**. Without delivery history the refusal moves read as arrogance, and the sources' only bridge is openly discounted work inside the focus [WWP:PROBONO] — slow and cash-hungry.
- **Enough demand and runway to let a deal die.** Under cash pressure the model is either abandoned or used dishonestly; the first source names cash pressure as the invalidating condition for its own mission statement [WWP:MISSIONOBJ].  <!-- privacy-ok: термин из книжного конспекта, не финансы владельца -->
- **A buyer senior enough to own value.** A Value Conversation with a process-runner is described as fruitless [WWP:JUNIOR-FALLBACK], [WWP:REGROUP].
- **ACV high enough to pay for the process.** Below the band where a Value Conversation returns its cost, [WWP:FOUR-Q], [WWP:ORDER-MAG] and [WWP:ANCHOR-CLOSED] cost more than they produce.
- **A process that can lawfully be bent.** In regulated and public procurement, [WWP:BID-LAST], [WWP:ORALPROP], [WWP:DEPOSIT], [WWP:BIGROUND] and most of the process-objection repertoire are unusable.
