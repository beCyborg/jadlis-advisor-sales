# advisor-khalsa — core

The method: ORDER (Opportunity → Resources → Decisions → Exact solution / Enable decisions →
Results), run iteratively across many conversations, with two disciplines on top — **No Guessing**
about Opportunity, Resources and Decisions, and **Slow Down for Yellow Lights**. The lens's
qualification metric is the free flow of meaningful, client-stated information.

Ranked below by how strongly each component is actually supported, strongest token first.
Everything methodological here is FranklinCovey practitioner material; the book's evidence
ceiling is `practitioner-n-many`, and no principle in this file reaches `field-study`.

---

## Tier 1 — `external-research`, but for the premise only, never for the technique

Three principles have a named outside source underneath them. In every case the source
supports the *context* (that the problem exists, that the base rate is what it is) and never
the effect of the book's own move. Cite them accordingly; a validator that reads these as
validation of the method is being misled.

1. **Referral beats cold contact for executive access.** The access ladder in [REAL:NOCOLD],
   [REAL:EASYNO], [REAL:TRIALRUN], [REAL:REFSCRIPT] rests on the Gardner/Bistritz/Klompmaker
   white paper: ~84% chance an executive agrees to talk when referred from inside her own
   company, ~44% when referred from outside, ~44% when met outside the office. The cited
   cold-call baseline (~100 calls → ~10 appointments → 1–2 sales) sits in the same tier.
   What is *not* externally supported: the claim that a referral can always be found, and
   every cadence number in [REAL:REFNETWORK].
2. **Account retention dominates service-business profitability.** [REAL:MEASURERETAIN] rests
   on the 1994 HBR piece (Shapiro, Slywotzky, Doyle) naming five determinants — retention,
   dominance from preferred-vendor position, realized price, selling and service cost,
   account selection — with retention the largest in service businesses. The operational
   advice built on it (keep measuring, run an account strategy) is the authors'.
3. **Decisions fail often enough that helping the client decide well is not a courtesy.**
   Paul Nutt's study of 356 companies (*Why Decisions Fail*) — more than half of decisions
   abandoned, only partially implemented, or never adopted — is the only substantial named
   external study in the book, and it is the strongest available support for the whole
   "help them reach a good decision, including no" premise behind [REAL:NOISOK],
   [REAL:TAKEAWAY] and [REAL:FORCEDECIDE].
   *Demoted third-party number:* the ES Research figures (65% of salespeople pursue worthless
   deals; 80% of lost opportunities trace to inadequate qualification) are third-party but
   unverified here — do not quote them as established.

## Tier 2 — `practitioner-n-many`: repeated across many engagements, still self-collected

This is where the load-bearing mass of the method sits. Repeated across thousands of
participants and hundreds of deals, taught in 40+ countries — but the observer, the trainer
and the beneficiary are the same organization. Strong for *transferable structure*, weak for
*claimed effect on win rate*.

4. **Qualification is the amount of client-stated information you hold, not your conviction.**
   [REAL:INFO-FLOW-QUAL], [REAL:SPI]. The anchor is a multi-billion-dollar scrub of ~400
   deals where sellers asserted urgent client problems but could not point to client words
   confirming importance, evidence or impact. This is the single most reusable idea in the
   lens and the one with the biggest observed base — still practitioner data.
5. **Move off the solution before discussing the solution.** [REAL:LSM], [REAL:ALL-ISSUES],
   [REAL:PRIORITIZE], [REAL:NOMOVE]. Taught with worked ERP and supply-chain dialogues;
   the accompanying failure taxonomy (wrong people / no real opportunity / your own
   communication / mandated purchase / already chosen) is field-derived, not measured.
6. **Words do not carry shared meaning; ask what the key word means.** [REAL:KEYWORD-MEANING].
   The word-overlap exercise (groups predict 3–4 shared synonyms for a common business term,
   actual overlap is almost always zero) has been run with tens of thousands of participants
   over years. Self-collected, but the *n* is real and the result is falsifiable in one meeting.
7. **Impact must be arithmetic the client owns.** [REAL:FIVE-GOLDEN], [REAL:ENVELOPE-MATH],
   [REAL:CLIENT-OWNS-MATH], [REAL:PEEL-ONION], [REAL:SOFT-EVIDENCE-QS], [REAL:SCALE10].
   Taught sequence with multiple worked dialogues. The rule "a number you own is your problem,
   not theirs" is the practical core; the 80/20 issue split and the 1–10 read thresholds
   (8–10 motivating, 7 keep talking, ≤6 yellow) are rules of thumb, unsourced.
8. **Yellow lights get cheaper the earlier they are named.** [REAL:YELLOW-SLOW],
   [REAL:STATE-OBVIOUS], [REAL:UNASKED-Q], [REAL:WWHTH], [REAL:UNPACKWORD], [REAL:YLPRECEPT].
   The anchor case is a billion-dollar outsourcing loss where the client said at the very
   first meeting they believed they could achieve the same savings in-house, and the objection
   went unaddressed for 18 months. One loss postmortem plus repeated coaching — persuasive
   mechanism, not a measured protocol. The Argyris framing (making the undiscussable
   discussable) is borrowed reasoning, not evidence.
9. **The decision process is qualified separately from the need, and learned from the people
   in it.** [REAL:GRID], [REAL:NOISOK], [REAL:MISSINGWHO], [REAL:ALTERNATIVES],
   [REAL:WINLOSE], [REAL:PERSONAL], [REAL:TWENTYQ], [REAL:NINEQ]. Named method, taught and
   used with clients, no outcome dataset. The sequencing rule — ask for the *steps* before
   asking who decides, because the "who decides" question corrupts the answer — is the
   sharpest and most testable claim in this cluster.
10. **Access is advocated for, not requested; refusal of access is data.**
    [REAL:ADVOCATE], [REAL:RECIPROC], [REAL:MATCHLEAD], [REAL:ROLEREV], [REAL:BACKDOOR],
    [REAL:BLOCKED4], [REAL:RFP-HUMAN-RULE]. The RFP rule has the best support in this cluster:
    one client's own 18-month audit — 46 unsolicited-RFP responses, ~$80K average direct cost,
    zero wins. That is one company's self-reported study, not a benchmark.
11. **Do not present until the preconditions exist; present to enable a decision.**
    [REAL:READYGATE], [REAL:ENDINMIND], [REAL:STEPSTONES], [REAL:CHECKOFF], [REAL:AGENDAPRE],
    [REAL:ENOUGHTIME], [REAL:STEPSPEC], [REAL:WEARETHEPROPOSAL]. Reported successful across
    diverse applications worldwide; no comparative data at all. The strongest sub-claim is
    negative and mechanism-based: information-rich, decision-poor presentations manufacture
    objections that would not otherwise have existed.
12. **Money is discussed as a range of what others invested, before any proposal work.**
    [REAL:MONEY3], [REAL:HOWNUMBER], [REAL:TIMEQ], [REAL:PEOPLESPLIT]. Reported to work in
    most cases; the counterexamples (consultants who spent more on a proposal than the
    client's entire budget) are the real argument. The Y ≤ X + 25–50% band is a rule of thumb.
13. **Price is a function of scope × timing × division of labor.** [REAL:SCOPELEVER],
    [REAL:PRICELAST], [REAL:PRICEEQUIV], [REAL:PLOYANS], [REAL:NODEALPT]. Standing framework
    with scripted exchanges. This is the thinnest chapter in the book: several of its
    supporting claims are attributed to unnamed negotiation research (see Tier 4).
14. **Preparation is a written artifact, not a state of mind.** [REAL:HIGHRISK5],
    [REAL:BCHYPO2], [REAL:INTENT-STMT], [REAL:ASSUMPTION-AUDIT], [REAL:LISTEN-EXACT],
    [REAL:QUESTHYG], [REAL:FEWQ], [REAL:INITYL], [REAL:THREEHEAR]. Repeated coaching
    exercises across many audiences. The visualization component inside HIGHRISK5 is
    asserted, not supported — do not carry the whole move at this tier.

## Tier 3 — `practitioner-anecdote`: one told case, usually a win

Real moves, thin support, and the sample is visibly survivorship-selected: the told cases end
in wins. Recommend these with the outcome distribution explicitly unknown.

15. **Take the solution away when impact is small** — [REAL:TAKEAWAY]. Sample phrasings and
    the authors' observations only. Functions as an incongruence detector: run as a gambit
    without genuine willingness to lose, it misfires worse than not running it.
16. **Decline to propose rather than guess** — [REAL:WALKTALK]. One Fortune 100 case with a
    favourable outcome (the board reversed the gatekeeper). The losing runs are not reported.
17. **Reorder the client's decision sequence** — [REAL:STEPSWAP]. One large coached deal,
    with the outcome attributed by the participants themselves.
18. **Decision maker or ratifier test** — [REAL:RATIFIER]. Two war stories. The "four or five
    of the last ten pushed back = real decision maker" threshold has no basis beyond them.
19. **Hold the line on best-and-final and terms** — [REAL:HOLDTERMS]. One multimillion-dollar
    deal of the authors' own firm.
20. **Pre-call the executive to test the hypothesis** — [REAL:PRECALL], [REAL:OPENSTMT],
    [REAL:AGENDAPRE], [REAL:CYCLESHORT], [REAL:RULE955], [REAL:SOURCEASCLIENT]. The NewLogo
    and We-Do-It narratives supply most of the initiating chapter's "evidence"; both are the
    authors' own engagements, both ended in wins.
21. **Redirect to find the real question** — [REAL:REDIRECT]. One anchor anecdote. The authors
    themselves hedge the underlying maxim ("the question they ask is never the real question")
    as overstatement for emphasis — carry the hedge with the move.
22. **Present only with the deciders present** — [REAL:RIGHTROOM]. One expensive told case
    (the Chicago→London flight home). Also: [REAL:IMPACTGAP], [REAL:BELIEF3],
    [REAL:INCUMBENT-HEADON], [REAL:OWNMEDICINE], [REAL:FACTFIX], [REAL:WRONGOK],
    [REAL:CLIENT-NUMBERS] — each a single worked example or dialogue.

## Tier 4 — `assertion`: stated without support; use only as heuristics

Say "this is asserted" out loud whenever these are recommended. Several are borrowed from
outside traditions with the borrowing, not the evidence, transferred.

23. **The four Vs congruence model** — [REAL:FOURVS]. Framework stated flat, no support.
24. **NLP resource-state anchoring before calls** — [REAL:ANCHORSTATE]. Explicitly derived
    from NLP authors (Laborde, Robbins), presented with no evidence of effect. Neuro-flavoured
    justification is refuted by design in this council; use the move, drop the mechanism story.
25. **Kept-commitments trust drill** — [REAL:COMMITDRILL]. Derived from *The Speed of Trust*;
    the neural-pathway framing is unsupported and the 30–40 day habit figure is folklore.
26. **Concession psychology** — [REAL:SLOWCEDE], and the "higher targets yield higher returns"
    half of [REAL:NODEALPT]. Attributed to unnamed negotiation research with no citable source.
    This is the weakest support attached to a move that costs real money if wrong.
27. **Written-Q&A mechanics** — [REAL:WRITTENQ]. Reasoned from communication mechanics only.
28. **Batching versus serial resolution of yellow lights** — [REAL:YLBATCH]. Explicitly a
    judgment call with nothing behind it.
29. **Pre-built metaphors** — [REAL:METAPREP]; **acknowledging phrases** — [REAL:ACKPHRASE];
    **discovery-to-delivery handoff** — [REAL:HANDOFF]; **context widening's effect on price
    sensitivity** — [REAL:CONTEXT2]; **method triage** — [REAL:METH-TRIAGE]. All stated as
    important, none supported.

## Tier 5 — beliefs, not principles

The five key beliefs under ORDER are stance, not evidence, and should be labelled as such
whenever they are used to justify a recommendation: consultant and client share an interest
in an exact fit; intent counts more than technique (Trust = Intent + Expertise; pushing
triggers reactance); solutions have no inherent value, only problems people care about or
results they highly value; the method fuses business thinking, emotional safety and a
repeatable process; world-class inquiry precedes world-class advocacy, against the three
default postures Tell / Accept / Guess.

The most consequential of these for this council is **intent over technique**: it is what
makes [REAL:TAKEAWAY], [REAL:WALKTALK], [REAL:PRICELAST] and [REAL:NODEALPT] work at all,
and it is unfalsifiable as stated. Treat it as an operating commitment the seller either
makes or does not, and judge the moves by whether the seller is genuinely willing to lose
the deal — not by whether the script was delivered correctly.
