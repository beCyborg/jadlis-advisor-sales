# advisor-negotiation — core principles, ranked by evidence

The negotiation layer is carried by a contradictory pair: the principled school
(`-GTY`, ceiling `external-research`) and the refusal-discipline school
(`-CAMP`, ceiling `practitioner-n-many`). This file ranks the layer's components by what
actually holds them up, strongest token first. The ranking is deliberately unflattering:
most of what is famous here is assertion-grade, and the strongest-supported items are
peripheral mechanics rather than the headline frameworks.

**Ladder:** `field-study` → `external-research` → `practitioner-n-many` →
`practitioner-anecdote` → `assertion`.

---

## Tier 0 — field-study: empty

Neither school offers a field study. There is no win-rate comparison, no control group, no
outcome data on deals run with the method against deals run without it. Any claim that this
layer "works" is an inference from mechanism plus self-report, and should be labelled as such
in a verdict.

---

## Tier 1 — external-research (four components, all `-GTY`, all narrow)

1. **Channel choice changes deception and impasse rates** — [NEGO:CHANNELPICK-GTY].
   The only quantified external result in the canon: sellers with private information lied in
   a small minority of face-to-face interactions, about a third of written ones and more than
   half of phone ones; mutually beneficial agreements reached in almost 60% face-to-face, 22%
   written, 38% phone; impasse in more than half of written exchanges against 19% face-to-face
   (Valley, Moag & Bazerman 1998; review in Nadler & Shestowsky 2006). Scope caveat: laboratory
   dyads, not enterprise deal cycles. This is the single strongest thing the layer knows.

2. **Reactive devaluation makes the first offer less mechanical than folklore says** —
   [NEGO:OFFERTIMING-GTY]. Credited to Curhan, Neale & Ross (2004): a proposal is discounted
   because of who made it. Supports anchoring on the *standard* rather than the number, and
   supports the refusal to hold either "always open first" or "always let them go first" as a
   rule. Note this is external research imported into a negotiation prescription, not research
   on the prescription.

3. **Commitment as a strategic device** — [NEGO:LOCKLOOSE-GTY]. Rests on Schelling's analysis
   of self-binding commitment. The *analysis* is external; the counter-move (interfere with the
   transmission of the commitment, de-emphasise so they can climb down) is the authors' own
   assertion sitting on top of it.

4. **Attack the problem while supporting the person** — [NEGO:HARDSOFT-GTY]. Grounded in
   cognitive-dissonance theory with no specific study or effect size cited. Demote in practice
   to the weakest end of this tier. The same applies to [NEGO:GETINSTEP-GTY], whose only cited
   source is Gilligan (1982) on gender — thin ground for a claim about cross-cultural
   adaptation — and to [NEGO:ATTACKNAME-GTY], whose eye-contact effect is credited to unnamed
   student experiments and should be read as practitioner-grade.

**What this tier does not contain:** BATNA, interests-behind-positions, objective criteria,
the four elements, mission and purpose, the budget model, "no is good", unokayness. None of
the layer's load-bearing frameworks reach this tier in either school.

## Tier 2 — practitioner-n-many (repeated cases, one observer, no counterfactual)

This is the ceiling of the counter-canon in full and the working middle of the canon.

- **Measure offers against a real alternative, not a bottom line** — [NEGO:BATNA-GTY]. The
  layer's most-used instrument and the most consequential thing at this tier. Supported by
  Harvard Negotiation Project casework taught across decades; no dataset. The critique embedded
  in it (bottom lines are arbitrary, set too high by whoever benefits, and suppress
  multi-variable packages) is reasoning, not measurement.
- **Prepare a menu of independent standards before the number** — [NEGO:PREPSTANDARDS-GTY],
  with [NEGO:REFRAME-GTY] referencing the wider reframing literature. Repeated across
  insurance, construction and real-estate practice.
- **Behaviour goals beat result goals; controllable activity is the only honest target** —
  [NEGO:GOAL-BEHAVIOR-ONLY-CAMP], [NEGO:PAYSIDE-TRIAGE-CAMP], [NEGO:DAILY-TRACK-CAMP].
  Coached across a large client base. The counterfactual is structurally unavailable: clients
  who abandoned the discipline are not in the sample.
- **Neediness is visible and priced by trained buyers** — [NEGO:WANT-NOT-NEED-CAMP], reported
  by clients as the highest-impact idea in the system in an informal self-reported poll.
  Mechanism plausible, measurement absent.
- **Question grammar transfers the work** — [NEGO:INTERROGATIVE-LED-CAMP],
  [NEGO:QUESTION-DISCIPLINE-CAMP], [NEGO:KILL-VERB-LED-CAMP], [NEGO:NO-TALKING-CAMP],
  [NEGO:NOTES-CAMP]. Standing coaching corrections with a logged call as illustration. This is
  the most transferable part of the counter-canon and the part least dependent on its
  adversarial premise.
- **Confirm agreements repeatedly before treating them as real** — [NEGO:THREE-PLUS-CAMP].
  Long-standing coaching rule; the accompanying claim that it cannot be overdone is
  unfalsifiable as stated and should be quoted as a rule, never as a finding.
- **Map and negotiate the decision path, not the contact** — [NEGO:DM-MAP-INTERROGATIVE-CAMP],
  [NEGO:PRESENT-QUALIFY-CAMP], [NEGO:BLOCKER-LADDER-CAMP], and on the canon side
  [NEGO:AUTHCHECK-GTY]. Both schools arrive here; it is the one structural claim on which the
  layer is internally consistent.
- **Negotiate the rules of the game when behaviour blocks substance** — [NEGO:RULEGAME-GTY],
  [NEGO:SETTINGFIX-GTY], [NEGO:FADEOPP-GTY]. Harvard mediation practice across labour, legal
  and diplomatic cases — note the domain gap from commercial selling.
- **Guard disclosure in informal settings** — [NEGO:BEAN-GUARD-CAMP]. Several client-side
  disclosures reported in substance; the detection cost of the posture is never priced.
- **A complete offer often settles the deal** — [NEGO:CLOSEMOVE-GTY]. Repeated observation
  from advisory practice; directly contradicted at this same tier by
  [NEGO:NO-CLOSING-CAMP] at assertion grade — see the collision list in `moves.md`.

## Tier 3 — practitioner-anecdote (single cases, often diplomatic or self-reported)

The bulk of the layer's memorable material sits here. Treat every number attached to these as
self-reported by an interested party.

- **Interests behind positions** — [NEGO:WHY-GTY]: the librarian/window story and Camp David.
  Load-bearing for the entire canon and supported by two anecdotes.
- **One-text procedure** — [NEGO:ONETEXT-GTY]: Camp David 1978, 13 days and roughly 23 drafts;
  the Law of the Sea prototype. Powerful mechanism, zero commercial evidence.
- **Dovetailing differences and package pairs** — [NEGO:DOVETAIL-GTY],
  [NEGO:PREFPAIRS-GTY], [NEGO:SCOPEVARY-GTY]: treaty and sports-agent illustrations.
- **Face-saving as a real interest** — [NEGO:FACESAVE-GTY]: the mayor who accepted identical
  terms once he could present them as his own decision.
- **Reopening a losing contract** — [NEGO:APOLOGY-REOPEN-CAMP], [NEGO:REOPEN-BAD-DEAL-CAMP],
  [NEGO:NEXT-DECISION-CAMP]: the Network, Inc. narrative, roughly $100,000 lost per machine,
  with all recovery figures reported by the coach who was paid for the engagement.
- **Refusing the price-down auction** — [NEGO:REFUSE-WHIPSAW-CAMP]: one supplier, one win, no
  base rate of suppliers who exited an auction and simply lost.
- **Full-rate asking** — [NEGO:FULL-RATE-ASK-CAMP]: a $100/hour expert asking $500/hour and
  being accepted in about 45 minutes. One case; survivorship unaddressed.
- **Deferring numbers raises price** — [NEGO:NUMBERS-LATE-CAMP]: one client's unit price
  moving from $185 to $290. A single data point doing very heavy work.
- **Concession stacking reads as delivery risk** — [NEGO:CONCESSION-SUSPICION-CAMP]: one lost
  deal, buyer's CEO's stated reasoning taken at face value.
- **Whose alternative is real** — [NEGO:WHETHERBATNA-GTY], [NEGO:THEIRBATNA-GTY],
  [NEGO:BATNADEV-GTY]: town-versus-corporation and community-versus-plant narratives.

## Tier 4 — assertion (mechanism argued, nothing measured)

Everything here is a *stance*. Useful, often sharp, but it must never be cited as if it were a
finding — and this tier includes several of the layer's most quotable rules.

- **Positional bargaining is a different game, and soft loses to hard** — the founding claim of
  [NEGO:METAGAME-GTY] and [NEGO:WISETEST-GTY]. Definitional.
- **Never yield to pressure, only to principle** — [NEGO:NOYIELD-GTY]. The claim that the
  principled side "generally has an edge" is unmeasured.
- **Abolish closing** — [NEGO:NO-CLOSING-CAMP]. Presented as a rule with war stories and no
  comparative close-rate data, while contradicting a Tier-2 canon observation.
- **Never save the adversary** — [NEGO:NO-SAVING-CAMP]. Mechanism argued from one retracted
  concession, and in open contradiction with the same book's own
  [NEGO:FAIR-VALUE-TOPUP-CAMP].
- **Unokayness and the esteem/okayness split** — [NEGO:NOT-OKAY-CAMP] (n-many),
  [NEGO:ESTEEM-VS-OKAY-CAMP] and [NEGO:PROP-FUMBLE-CAMP] (assertion / single anecdote). The
  theatrical end of this cluster is the layer's clearest ethical problem, not its evidence
  problem.
- **The positive strip line never backfires** — [NEGO:STRIPLINE-POS-CAMP]. A trainer's
  self-report of a null failure rate; treat as unfalsifiable, never quote the claim.
- **Deliberate vagueness is legitimate** — [NEGO:PLANT-ASSUMPTION-CAMP],
  [NEGO:NO-RISK-ANSWER-CAMP]. Asserted to work "most of the time" with no case; this lens
  additionally refuses them where a figure will be relied upon.
- **Budget weights (time 1x, energy 2x, money 3x, emotion 4x, compounding to 24x)** —
  [NEGO:BUDGET-TRIPLE-CAMP]. Explicitly admitted by the author to be non-empirical. The useful
  content is the sunk-cost mechanism; the arithmetic is decoration.
- **Warnings beat threats, offers beat threats** — [NEGO:WARNVSTHREAT-GTY],
  [NEGO:OFFERNOTTHREAT-GTY]. Reasoning plus scripts.
- **Standards as sword and shield** — [NEGO:LEGITSHIELD-GTY]. Argued by analogy to legal
  precedent.
- **Emotion work** — [NEGO:EMOAUDIT-GTY], [NEGO:IDENTITY-GTY], [NEGO:STEAM-GTY],
  [NEGO:SYMBOL-GTY], [NEGO:PAIN-CALIBRATE-CAMP]. Diagnostic frameworks handed to untrained
  negotiators, described from practice, none measured.
- **Adoption cost** — the ~800 hours to adjust attributed to "one study I give credence to"
  with no named source is bare assertion and must never be repeated as a number.

## What the ranking implies for using this layer

1. **The layer's confidence should be structural, not empirical.** Recommend a move because
   its mechanism fits the deal profile, not because it is proven — nothing here is proven.
2. **Strongest-supported ≠ most important.** The best-evidenced components (channel choice,
   reactive devaluation) are tactical; the frameworks a verdict will lean on (alternatives,
   interests, mission, budget) are Tier 2–3.
3. **Both books monetise the method they document.** The canon sells training and a
   negotiation programme; the counter-canon's diagnosis routes explicitly to the author's own
   coaching. Self-reported wins are marketing artefacts before they are evidence.
4. **Agreement between the two schools is one layer speaking twice.** Where GTY and CAMP
   converge (see the closing section of `moves.md`), that is informative because it is rare —
   but it is still a single layer, never two independent confirmations.
5. **Where the two collide, the evidence does not adjudicate.** Both sides of every collision
   in `moves.md` are Tier 2–4. The tiebreaker is the deal profile and the observable signal
   named there, not the strength of the citation.
