# advisor-negotiation — myths, demotions and documented failure modes

What this layer refuses, and what it refuses *inside itself*. Two opposed schools sit here, so
several entries are demotions of one school made by the other — those are marked, because a
demotion from a rival school is an argument, not a finding.

---

## 1. Myths this layer debunks (both schools agree on the target)

- **"Be nicer and you'll get a better deal."** Soft and hard positional bargaining are the same
  game at different volumes; soft loses to hard reliably. The fix is a different game, not a
  midpoint. Debunked from opposite directions by [NEGO:METAGAME-GTY] and
  [NEGO:WINWIN-TRAP-CAMP].
- **"Split the difference."** The characteristic failure product of positional bargaining.
  Splitting between two arbitrary figures produces an arbitrary result; only splitting between
  figures each backed by an independent standard is defensible — [NEGO:FAIRPROC-GTY].
- **"Ask them to be more forthcoming."** Reliably fails: whatever they concede becomes your new
  floor.
- **"Always make the first offer" / "always let them go first."** Both rejected. Preparation
  dominates both, firmness on an early figure is a credibility trap, and taking your sense of
  value from their opening number is called extremely risky — [NEGO:OFFERTIMING-GTY].
- **"Wealth, size and clout are power."** They are not, until converted into an actual
  alternative; visible wealth can actively weaken a buyer. Asking who is more powerful is
  harmful in both directions — believing you are stronger breeds under-preparation, believing
  you are weaker breeds discouragement, and preferring to feel powerless is named as a
  comfortable avoidance of responsibility.
- **"They rubber-stamp whatever I bring."** Almost nobody decides alone; national stereotypes
  of decision structure (vertical versus horizontal) are rejected as mixed and unpredictable —
  [NEGO:DM-MAP-INTERROGATIVE-CAMP], [NEGO:AUTHCHECK-GTY].
- **"Reading about it builds the skill."** Both schools say the discipline is trained like a
  sport, in low-stakes repetitions — [NEGO:LOWRISKLADDER-GTY], [NEGO:RULE-DRILL-CAMP]. "Who's
  winning?" is named as the wrong question.

## 2. The counter-canon's demotion of the canon (CAMP → GTY) — an argument, not evidence

- **"Win-win" is treated as a mood, not a method.** The charge: it gives no basis for judging
  whether 27% or 24% was the right discount, and a win-win trophy handed over by a large
  customer is evidence of a failed negotiation.
- **The canon's "wise agreement" test is attacked as undefinable** — who decides which
  interests are legitimate, or what "fair", "durable" and "community interests" mean — and as
  smuggling compromise in under a neutral word. This lands on [NEGO:WISETEST-GTY] specifically.
- **Cooperative framing is read as an extraction instrument** — [NEGO:WINWIN-TRAP-CAMP] against
  [NEGO:SIDEBYSIDE-GTY].
- **Counter-demotion, for symmetry:** the canon's own answer is that this reading assumes
  extraction everywhere and cannot describe a counterpart genuinely trying to build something
  jointly. Neither charge is settled by data. Log both, pick by deal profile, never average.

## 3. Demoted inside the canon (GTY demoting its own popular reading)

- **"Separate the people from the problem" ≠ ignore feelings.** The authors considered
  replacing "separate" with "disentangle" precisely because readers misused it; people problems
  often need *more* attention than substantive ones, not less.
- **"Face-saving" is not a derisive term.** It is a real interest, not a courtesy —
  [NEGO:FACESAVE-GTY].
- **Objective criteria ≠ *your* criterion.** Standards used as ammunition for a position you
  already hold is the common counterfeit, and escalating a practical disagreement into "a
  matter of principle" is a failure mode, not a virtue — [NEGO:OPENJUDGE-GTY].
- **Bottom lines are demoted outright.** Arbitrary, usually set too high by whoever benefits,
  they close your ears to what you learn and suppress creative packages. Seeing your
  alternatives "in the aggregate" is named as a cognitive error — you can only have one.
- **Negotiators are more often over-committed to agreeing than over-optimistic.** Postponing
  the "what if there is no deal" question is negotiating blind.
- **Third parties do not automatically help.** A mediator who asks about positions and presses
  for concessions deepens attachment; assembling fragments of many proposals is dismissed.
- **Inferring their intentions from your fears** feels safe and plays well to spectators but
  blinds you to small moves toward agreement.
- **The trade-off between a good outcome and a good relationship is denied**, as is the framing
  that you must choose between getting what you deserve and being decent — but the book also
  disclaims being a sermon: it does not tell you to accept the first arguably-fair offer.

## 4. Demoted inside the counter-canon (CAMP demoting standard sales practice and itself)

- **Closing.** Trial closes, manufactured urgency and "close, close, close" exhortation are
  named as the behaviour sophisticated counterparts punish; a signed deal proves nothing about
  whether it was the best deal — [NEGO:NO-CLOSING-CAMP].
- **"No burns bridges."** Inverted: the inability to accept a refusal is what burns them.
  "Maybe" claims to protect against a wrong decision and delivers paralysis; an easy early yes
  is a trap later qualified away — [NEGO:INVITE-NO-CAMP], [NEGO:KILL-MAYBE-CAMP],
  [NEGO:EARLY-YES-ALARM-CAMP].
- **"Positive attitude."** Reframed as disguised positive expectation and therefore a liability;
  an excited buyer is a remorse risk — [NEGO:BLANK-SLATE-CAMP]. This is the sharpest break with
  conventional sales coaching in the layer.
- **Quantitative goals are called dangerous even when met** — hitting the number early breeds
  coasting, missing it breeds harder work on the wrong activity.
- **"Creating pain" is called amateur hour.** The negotiator builds the counterpart's *vision*
  of a problem that already exists; and knowing their problem while they do not is not one-up —
  an unaware counterpart has no incentive to decide.
- **Presentations.** The claim is that effective negotiations survive presentations but never
  require them; a deck puts the counterpart into critique mode and manufactures objections,
  and production values cannot rescue content aimed at the wrong problem. Allowing "maybe" as a
  presentation outcome converts it into a free option for the buyer.
- **Warm referrals** are named as the fastest route to losing discipline —
  [NEGO:WARM-AS-COLD-CAMP].
- **Budget as a line-item cost sheet** is demoted: money is one of three budgets and the
  cheapest to lose; sunk cost is the mechanism that corrupts decisions.
- **Topic-list and hidden agendas** are demoted: topic lists get abandoned in practice, and
  deals produced by ambush do not stick.

## 5. Numbers that must never be quoted as fact

Everything in this section is self-reported by an author with a commercial interest in the
method, or unsourced in the original.

- **Counter-canon practice scale (2002):** ~20 years coaching, ~15,000 individuals, ~150
  corporations, >$4 billion in transactions over a decade, ~750 negotiations a year. Author's
  own accounting; no independent verification, and clients who abandoned the system are absent
  from the sample.
- **Budget weights** time 1x / energy 2x / money 3x / emotion 4x, compounding 2x → 6x → 24x.
  Explicitly admitted by the author as illustrative and non-empirical. Quote the sunk-cost
  mechanism, not the multipliers.
- **~800 hours to adopt the system**, attributed to "one study I give credence to" with no named
  source. Bare assertion. Do not repeat as a figure.
- **Prospecting rates** — roughly 3 good appointments per 10 cold calls; 30 trained
  salespeople producing 90 valid appointments after 18 hours of training. Self-reported
  training-programme output.
- **Deal outcomes** — $400,000 opening ending at $8 million; a $30 million order at full price
  after three days of refusal; a 28% discount reopened and mostly recovered; unit price $185
  before the method and $290 after; a $1.7M list versus a $1.3M demanded volume price; the
  Network, Inc. turnaround (~$100,000 lost per machine, a claimed recovery in the tens of
  millions and $100M in new orders). Each is a single case reported by the coach who was paid
  for it, with no counterfactual.
- **"The positive strip line has never backfired."** A claimed null failure rate from a trainer.
  Structurally unfalsifiable and commercially motivated.
- **"Decisions are 100 percent emotional."** Bare assertion presented as a law.
- **Canon-side unmeasured superiority claims** — that principled negotiation "usually"
  dominates, that the principled side "generally has an edge", that outcomes are better "in
  virtually every case". None measured.
- **"Aspiration level correlates strongly with result"** (canon) — studies invoked, no source
  named. Treat as assertion.
- **Historical set pieces used as if they were negotiation data** — Camp David, Trieste 1954,
  the Law of the Sea, Malta 1971, Ho Chi Minh's table shape, Vietnam bombing pauses, a scene
  from a feature film. They illustrate mechanisms; they establish nothing about commercial
  deals. The "Japanese always research" generalisation is an unsupported stereotype and should
  be struck rather than repeated.

## 6. Anti-recommendations of this lens (moves the layer carries but will not prescribe)

These are in `moves.md` for coverage. The lens declines to recommend them, and says why.

- **[NEGO:PLANT-ASSUMPTION-CAMP]** — deliberately inducing a false belief about your price via
  elastic words, admitted by the source to be asymmetric by design. Refused wherever a figure
  will be relied on: reputational exposure, and in regulated contexts legal exposure. The
  ethical contour of this council ("would you show the buyer this tactic?") kills it outright.
- **[NEGO:NO-RISK-ANSWER-CAMP]** — contentless answers claimed to work "most of the time".
  Refused where trust is the binding constraint; it is the clearest candidate in the layer for
  reading as dishonest once detected.
- **[NEGO:PROP-FUMBLE-CAMP]** and the theatrical end of **[NEGO:NOT-OKAY-CAMP]** /
  **[NEGO:COLUMBO-DRILL-CAMP]** — staged incompetence to trigger a helping reflex. Marked as
  theatre rather than candour; unusable where credibility is the product, and corrosive if
  recognised.
- **[NEGO:BUDGET-BUILD-THEIRS-CAMP]** — manufactured callback friction and imposed rework.
  Deliberate cost imposition on a counterparty; the source itself concedes it is pointless
  against anyone trained in the same system, and never prices detection.
- **[NEGO:PUSHBACK-DECISION-CAMP] used on a vulnerable buyer** — the source's own case is an
  elderly client committing a large share of her savings. If the invitation to refuse is not
  real, the move is manipulation wearing the costume of integrity.
- **[NEGO:BENEFIT-IN-QUESTION-CAMP] on an unproven premise** — "how can you survive without X"
  with nothing behind it is pressure with the tone of inquiry.
- **[NEGO:RESEARCH-CAMP] at the personal-details end** — the source's own illustration of
  applied research is one the author himself calls horrible. Opposition research on individuals
  is an ethical boundary, not a checklist item.
- **[NEGO:BATNASHIFT-GTY] by coercive means** — flagged dual-use by its own authors; worsening
  their alternative through litigation threats or resource denial fails the canon's own
  reciprocity and legitimacy test.

## 7. Documented failure modes (reported inside the sources themselves)

- A plain refusal triggering a thirty-minute monologue about disrespect — conceded as the most
  common early reaction to [NEGO:INVITE-NO-CAMP].
- A team member unilaterally conceding 2% in a billion-dollar negotiation to break a silence;
  retracted the next day — the case behind [NEGO:NO-SAVING-CAMP].
- An internal colleague turning saboteur out of a desire for the counterpart's approval —
  [NEGO:TEAM-LEAK-WATCH-CAMP].
- An idea spilled without an agenda and taken to a third party who inserted himself as broker —
  [NEGO:WANT-PROTECT-IDEA-CAMP].
- A technical evaluator defending roughly $100M of installed equipment by sabotaging meetings —
  the case behind [NEGO:SABOTAGE-PROBE-CAMP].
- A sales manager's closing instinct nearly breaking a completed decision —
  [NEGO:OWN-SIDE-PRENEG-CAMP].
- A field rep letting a purchasing contact cancel a worldwide service contract; the supplier
  absorbed the blame when service degraded — [NEGO:QUALIFY-DECIDER-CAMP].
- Canon-side: appeasement teaching stubbornness (Munich 1938), extreme demands killing deals
  through lost credibility, lock-ins forcing a humiliating climbdown when called, and attacking
  a person for using a tactic making the tactic harder to abandon.

## 8. Internal contradictions the layer must not smooth over

- **The counter-canon bans saving the adversary and then prescribes topping up a supplier who
  lost money** — [NEGO:NO-SAVING-CAMP] versus [NEGO:FAIR-VALUE-TOPUP-CAMP]. The source does not
  resolve it. This lens confines the second to post-delivery, buyer-side use only.
- **The canon condemns concealment in positional bargainers and then advises withholding your
  flexibility** — [NEGO:PURPOSE-GTY] and [NEGO:CANDIDGAP-GTY] against the transparency claim.
- **The counter-canon prefers email to reduce emotional leakage while its whole delivery model
  rests on tonal warmth** — [NEGO:NO-TALKING-CAMP] against [NEGO:NURTURE-CAMP]. Never
  reconciled.
- **"You never need this deal" is false for the undercapitalised seller the book addresses.**
  A psychology fix is offered for a capital problem; the honest version is to fix the
  dependency, not to perform indifference.
- **The counter-canon is self-sealing.** Doubting the positive strip line is diagnosed as being
  stuck in closing mode; the confirmation ritual is claimed to be impossible to overdo. Both
  immunise the technique against disconfirmation and should be named as such in any verdict.
